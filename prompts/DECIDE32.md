# DECIDE32: the defaults NIGHT32 builds on (GG can reverse any; each is one design.n32 row or number)

- **D-1 The grapple's limit:** 2 charges, each back after 4 s, at least 0.8 s between pulls, a non-boss kill gives one back, landing does not refill, a press with no target is free, tap and hold both spend one, a cancel keeps at most 20 m/s. Research said 2 charges / 6 s (M2) or 2 / 3 s with landing refill (A1); 4 s without landing refill is the middle. Alternatives: 1 charge / 5 s (kills cut 2 s); Apex-style cooldown by distance.
- **D-2 Boss length:** first 45-75 s for the Player pilot with the level's weapons (GG: "defeat in like a minute"). REVISED 20:10 after GG: "bosses still too tanky!": the pilot band is now 25-40 s (harness p50 about 30 s, p90 at most 45 s; pistol + fist bot at most 90 s), because the bot aims almost perfectly and a person with a pad takes about twice as long.
- **D-3 What a TAP pulls to:** rings, boss joints and big enemies, not bare walls (any wall in 60 m was a target: part of "too broken"). A HOLD can still swing from a wall.
- **D-4 Boss identities become bonuses:** the Lantern's charged-shot shield break, the Stag's open back, the Wyrm's head punch and the Loom's joints each give x2.5 for 3 s or more; outside them every weapon deals at least 25 %.
- **D-5 The round ring:** 64 drawn and solid facets over the Lantern's 16 logical sections (the fight's vents, sweep and platforms keep their places). Measured (harness ring32, 8 starts): a full wall-ride lap keeps 3-6 % less mean speed than the 16 flat panels (the Mover ends a ride 30 degrees round a curve and the speed sags to the ride floor), while each seam keeps 95 % against 73 %; 48 facets come out even on the lap. Kept 64 because GG asked for a circle; 48 is the alternative (one number: design.n32.arenas32.facetsPerPanel 3).
- **D-6 Level 5:** THE SPIRE (id n32-spire), a tower that remixes levels 1-4 one verb per room, a gauntlet, then a new boss, THE CROWN, in a true circle. GG owns enemy design: THE CROWN is a proposal built behind design.n32.boss5 (off = no level-5 boss).
- **D-7 Levels 1-3:** the rebuilt files are new ids (n32-foundry, n32-iceline, n32-kiln) swapped in by design.n32.arenas32; the n30 files stay in the tree.
- **D-8 Level 5's weapon:** the TORCH if it works in the campaign today (the old unlock table's n 5), else none.
