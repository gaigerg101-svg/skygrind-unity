<design_brief>
**Arena standard (R1 rules 1-11, `test` numbers):** a level is 3 arena beats at most, each 1-2 waves; wave size 6-12, never more than 15 alive; 35-55 kills per level; at most 2 enemy kinds per wave (3 with one heavy); the next wave starts when the group clears (not a timer); a room is at most 24 m wide (short axis) and 40 m long, 8-14 m tall, with two wall lanes; a full crossing takes about 3 s at wall-ride speed; jungle-gym tiers split ranged sightlines; ONE route enemies can climb always exists. Enemy counts come from a COST BUDGET in a Unity-side config (`design.n27.budget`: wave points = 10 + 3 x wave index; costs per kind husk 1, swarmer 0.5, spitter 2, heavy 6: `test`; a Unity-side file, say why the web has no such number). The plan's waves are generated from the budget and then hand-trimmed; the validator rejects a wave over the budget.
**Remove the random limits:** `pistolOnly`, `noDash`, `noJump` leave `layer1` and `level3` rooms. A limit may remain ONLY where a boss or lesson needs it, with the reason shown on screen in the room's banner (18 px+): level 2 is the one place the loadout is cut (the gauntlet only) and its intro says why.
**Wall lanes (R1 rules 7-10, `test`):** both long walls carry a 3-4 m tall wall-ride lane (cyan strip, `wall-ride-feel` guide from NIGHT26); the lane is the shortest line; the floor under it carries a hazard strip in the fast lane and a safe floor beside the slow lane (wall = fast and safe from melee, ground = slow and exposed). The validator computes both routes and the room fails if the wall route is not 30 % shorter in time (`levelgen` prints the ratio; NIGHT26's flue was 3.2 s vs 8.6 s).
**The tutorial (tutorial27), one lesson each, nothing repeated:** 1 move + look (a corridor); 2 jump + a pad hop; 3 dash across an island gap; 4 wall jump up a shaft; 5 WALL RIDE hall (the first lane, no damage, a safe-form hazard strip you can step on once); 6 slide under a gap; 7 PISTOL and its charge (RT hold on 3 targets); 8 PULSE (the 2.5 s swing; five targets die; the pips fill; LT blast launches you over a gap); 9 ONE fight room (8 enemies, 2 kinds, shotgun unlocked here); 10 THE LANTERN. The old s4-s7 husk rooms (four fights teaching the same thing) merge into stage 9. Par total at most 180 s including the boss.
**THE LANTERN (level 1 boss; R2 concept 1, the verb is WALL RIDE; the weapon is any gun for the exposed seconds and the PULSE to finish):** a hovering lantern-box in the middle of a ring-shaped courtyard (a ring of wall segments you can ride, 30-34 m across, roofed with a skylight). It keeps a shield plate turned to the player. THE SHIELD IS DOWN ONLY on the face that looks at a wall the player is currently RIDING (one flag derived from the Mover's wall-ride state and the wall id; a unit test in EditMode, no scene). It fires slow, readable orange orbs around the ring (telegraph 0.8 s the first attack, 0.6 s later; each attack has its own floor decal shape; red-orange = danger, cyan = the lit weak face). The player causes the window; the boss never opens it on a timer; a missed window costs time, not health; no stun-lock. Phases (one new attack each, never a new mechanic in the last): P1 one cyan wall lane + one orb pattern; P2 two wall lanes alternate (ride, hop to the other) + orbs from two heights; P3 the wall segments retract in sequence so you chain rides around the ring, and the finisher is a PULSE launch into the exposed face when the 5-kill charge is full (the boss drops five small SENTRIES in P3 that fill the pips: this teaches the kill-charge). Fight 75-100 s for the pistol bot-with-rides median; hurtable only on the lit face; HP bar with the three marks. Why it is not the Cerberus: the boss never stuns itself; you cause every window with a movement verb.
**THE GLACIER, level 2 as a level (R2 rules 11-17; the verb is SKATE + PULSE-LAUNCH; the weapon is the gauntlet only):** the level has three parts. (1) INTRO, 40 s: a short ice hall where the loadout is cut to the gauntlet (the banner says why), low friction, boost strips, 4-6 husks and swarmers to hit with the PULSE (kill-charge pips fill), then a boost-strip run onto the course. (2) THE FORCED WALL RIDE, 30 s: a stretch of damaging floor (hazard strip live 8 s) with ride-able walls: you must ride to the other end. (3) THE CHASE: the course is the 40 m tile ring (6 tiles, each seeded from (runSeed, tileIndex), recycled) in STRAIGHT track space; the VIEW curves it (Option A: the camera and tile meshes bend with heading change <= ~22 degrees per tile; the sim and collider stay straight so golden and replays are untouched); the boss skates ahead; its speed = base + k x (gap - target), clamped to +-15 % of the player's top speed (gap-based, not a timer); a HUD gap tape in metres + a cyan marker ring on the boss so it is never a speck; FOV push; floor streaks. **Hit rule:** when the gap < 12 m a PULSE-ready flash shows and a 1.5 s window opens; a PULSE-launch that lands on the boss counts; a miss costs 20 m of gap, never health; three hits kill (phase marks). Attacks are LANE attacks (3 lanes: a floor decal 0.7-0.8 s, then the low bar, the sweep or the spike wall arrives) plus one new one per phase (a roller; a ceiling icicle fall that you dodge by riding the wall); at most three kinds in the first version. The course ends 120-150 s after the chase starts at most. **The body: "the original" (GG).** NIGHT26's hull + engine block drawn from the sim's boxes is rejected. D-3 below decides what "original" is; the default is a large blocky skater in the web's blocky enemy style (rebuilt as the web's models are, rule 7), on one blade, keeping the current glow / aura / trail effects.
**Skate feel (skate27; R3 topic 2, all `test`):** speed-based FOV 0 at run speed to +10 at 1.5 x run speed (ease in 0.15 s, out 0.4 s, cap base + 12); camera roll = clamp(lateralSpeed / max, -1, 1) x 4 deg (6 on a slide), 0.12 s smoothing, render only; eye 0.05-0.08 m lower in a slide; a FLOOR STREAK shader on the ice (world-space lines along the velocity direction, 2.5 lines per metre, width 0.04, scroll k = speed x 0.3, strength 0 at 30 % of top speed to 1 at 100 %, two parallax layers, no textures; cheap URP unlit); 40-80 ice dust motes near the feet opposite to travel; a 6-10 particle carve spray on a heading change above 60 deg; two thin additive blade trails (0.25 s); a tiny camera shake (0.002 rad at top speed, 8-12 Hz). NO screen-border streaks. Measured: the floor-pixel motion (pixels per frame) rises at least 25 % over plain floor at matched speed; judged blind and paired.
**Slow-mo on a wall-ride aim (slowAim27; R1 rules 12-15):** one driver in the loop (`SurvivalGame`): `slow01` eases to 1 while (wall-riding AND aiming) and back to 0; the accumulator's dt is multiplied by `Lerp(1, 0.75, slow01)`; `Time.timeScale` is NOT touched; mouse / stick look reads the real frame delta, so aim stays crisp; the cue is one global Volume blended by `slow01`; hitstop wins over the slow. Also a pad tuning row. Not in the survival modes unless the row says so (default: campaign and survival both, one switch each).
**Roof and indoor look (roof27; R3 topic 3 option C):** a ceiling at (plan max height + 3 m), dusk-dark violet, over every room and link; a skylight slot of 20-30 % of the floor over boss arenas (GG: open is also fine; the slot is the compromise); vertex AO baked into the generated mesh colours (0.6 at inside corners, 0.8 along edges, 1.0 in the open, falloff 0.6 m); 6-10 emissive strips per room (cyan = player-usable, pale violet-white = boundary, red-orange only for hazards and enemies); at most 4 real point lights near the player; gradient ambient + exponential fog. The ceiling is a view mesh; the sim collider is unchanged (the player already cannot leave; if a ceiling collider exists in the plan it is the web collider's). Cost measured (frame ms with the roof ON vs OFF, 24 enemies).
**Open source, with licences (R1-R3; copy only MIT / Apache / CC0 / Unlicense and record each in `docs/THIRD-PARTY.md`: URL, licence, what was taken):** `Minoteaur / UnityHFSM` (MIT, read): the hierarchical state machine pattern for the boss FSM, rewritten in pure C# in `Skygrind.Core` (no UnityEngine; one transition per step; `needsExitTime` for attack states; timers in whole steps via `stepsFor`; rolls from Mulberry32); `UnitySpline2D` and `unity-curves` (MIT): arc-length tables for the curving course's VIEW, math only, ported with `JsMath` where it touches the sim; `CoffweDev / physics-based-wallrunning-unity` (MIT, Rigidbody based, parameters only: a cross-check of wall-ride feel, never copied); `mirzabeig / anime-speed-lines` (Unlicense): reference only, the border effect is BANNED by GG.
