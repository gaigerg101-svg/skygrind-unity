<design_brief>
**The room standard (the NIGHT27 standard tightened, because GG found 24 x 38 m still too wide):** a fight room is closed (a roof in the view and the collider; the `roof27` sky gap fixed), 14-20 m wide, 20-30 m long, 6-12 m tall; a ONE-verb room, 2-6 minutes of play for a level of 5-7 rooms; 12-30 kills in a room from the cost budget (`design.n27.budget`: husk 1, swarmer 0.5, spitter 2, heavy 6); every room has at least one surface that is faster than the floor (a wall lane, a rail, an ice strip) and at least one thing a standing player loses to (a hazard strip, a ranged enemy with a lane, a shield that opens only to a verb). Between fights an EMPTY REST ROOM (a short lit corridor or alcove, 6-10 m, a pickup or a prompt, no enemies: R1's rule; ULTRAKILL's 0-4 has three, 0-5 has four before the boss). Rooms differ from each other in shape (an L, a ring, a shaft, a pit with a bridge, a crossing) not only in size; no two consecutive rooms share a footprint. Doors: frames flush to the walls, no gap (item 5); lights: authored sockets only (item 2); props: authored positions only (item 3).
**Light (item 9):** closed rooms lit by the glow GG liked: the cyan and green neon trim on dark walls, wall-mounted and ceiling-recessed, plus ONE coloured accent light per room and a pool of light at the room's goal; red-orange only for danger (CLAUDE.md rule 8); pale violet-white for the boundary; a room is dark at its edges and bright where the fight is.
**The forcing devices** (use these, not locked doors): HAZARD FLOOR (the floor damages in cycles: you must be on a wall or a platform; the Lantern's attack and the tutorial's wall-ride room), THE GAP (a dash or pad crossing between two fight platforms), THE LOW CEILING (slide), THE SKATE LANE (item 13), THE ONE-WAY WALL (a lane that only exists in one direction), THE SHIELD THAT OPENS TO A VERB (headshot, punch, pulse, wall-ride aim), THE SPLASH ROOM (a crowd the rocket or the shotgun clears and the pistol cannot in time), THE FAR SHELF (a ranged enemy out of the shotgun's reach: the rail or pistol).
**Weapons and enemies (GG: a new weapon each level; the pistol still useful):** the awarded weapon appears at a level's START or inside its first room as a pickup, never from a LIMIT room; the level's enemies include one the new weapon is right against and one it is wrong against (`design.n26.vulnerability`, with a `_why`). A coloured TANK enemy in level 2 dies to one pulse-gauntlet hit, takes pistol damage at a small fraction, and the room says so (item 11). Default order (D-2): level 1 pistol (KESTREL) + the fist and PULSE gauntlet, nothing else; level 2 the same (the unlock is the boss's room); level 3 THUNDERCLAP (shotgun); then NAILGUN, TORCH, ZAPPER, RAIL, BOUNCER, ROCKET one per level, levels 4-10.
