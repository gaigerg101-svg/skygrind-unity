<design_brief_the_curriculum>
GG: "work mechanic to mechanic". The rule for every stage and level: **one new thing, taught in safety, tested in a fight, then combined.** The tutorial is not a text wall: the world teaches (a gap you must jump, a low tunnel you must slide, a target that needs a shot), a small prompt names the input once (pad glyph on a pad, 18 px or more, gone after first use: reuse the first-fifteen prompt system), a door opens when the lesson is done (a checkpoint at each door; the old `directed` cue points at it).
**Layer 1 = TUTORIAL (6 stages, each a small closed room or a link, about 6-8 minutes in all, no death fail state: a miss respawns you at the stage's start):**
Every stage uses the triplet **Exposition (the obstacle in view + one prompt line) > Validation (do it safely; the door stays shut until the lesson clears) > Challenge (a short gated proof)**, and no enemy is in the room while a movement verb is taught (stages 1.1-1.3). The first fight has ONE enemy kind (the husk); the first ranged enemy (the spitter) arrives in level 3. A defeated boss returns as a regular enemy later (the worm's tail pieces; the MONOLITH's husks).
1.1 LOOK AND WALK (an empty hall; a door at the far end; nothing else; the prompt names move + look).
1.2 JUMP AND SLIDE (a pit to jump over; a low tunnel to slide under; both with a recovery floor so no soft-lock).
1.3 WALL AND AIR (a wall-jump shaft, a jump-pad hop, a dash across a gap: one verb per alcove; the dash has a long cooldown: CORE.md).
1.4 THE PISTOL (the KESTREL on a pedestal; 3 static training targets, then 3 slow husks; the reticle arc appears here: teach the cooldown bar).
1.5 THE FIST (the PULSE GAUNTLET: melee a husk while holding fire: teaches melee-and-fire; the pulse charge builds by hits; a flick launches you over a gap that only a charged flick crosses).
1.6 FIRST FIGHT (a room with 2 waves of husks: movement + pistol + fist together; a checkpoint; the exit door opens on clear). Ends with the BIG PICK? No: the first pick comes at the end of level 2.
**Levels 2-10 (defaults; the lead may reorder with a logged reason; each ends with a boss or mini-boss beat or a gauntlet, one new enemy kind or verb per level):**
2 THE KILN (today's layer 1, trimmed: rooms + limits: PISTOL ONLY, NO DASH; earns the THUNDERCLAP; ends with THE KILN WYRM, now with a boss bar) | 3 SPITTERS AND COVER (enemy kind: spitter; teaches strafing and cover; LIMIT NO JUMP) | 4 VERTICAL (drones, a tall room, the grapple + ARCLINE; teaches air control; every tier reachable by enemies) | 5 CHARGERS AND LANCERS (crowd control, the slide-kick; LIMIT NO SLIDE; the pulse charge matters) | 6 THE BULWARK (built, off: wire it as a boss; shield-break teaches the headshot/melee break) | 7 WARDENS (area denial; LIMIT MELEE ONLY room; a mini-gauntlet of every enemy so far) | 8 SPEED (a timed run: par times matter; the highway STUB is allowed here only if everything else is DONE) | 9 THE MONOLITH (wire the first slice as a boss, finishing the cut items if time) | 10 FINALE (a mix of every verb and enemy; a difficulty spike in GG's "V2" sense: one duel room, no adds; the final boss: the WYRM's big brother or the rival, designed in `DESIGN24-bosses.md`).
**The pipeline's job:** a level = one plan file + `levelgen` + `LevelValidate24` + a bot run that prints per-room clear rate (60-90 %) and par; adding level N must cost one plan file and one config row. A `CURRICULUM.md` table lists each stage / level: the new thing, how the world teaches it, the prompt, the LIMIT flags, the bot's par, the enemy list, the pass check. The lead reads it before approving each level.
**Difficulty rule:** each level's first room is easier than the last room of the one before it (a breath), and the new thing appears alone before it is mixed.
