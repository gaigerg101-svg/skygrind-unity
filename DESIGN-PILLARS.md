**SKYGRIND in one line (GG, 2026-10-03):** "cod zombies meets ultrakill as the main, but other games should be inspired":
a first-person movement shooter at dusk where a solo run of rounds lasts 20-30 minutes to an exfil.

## The five pillars

1. **Movement is the moat.** The web game's movement, ported bit for bit (golden vectors, 22 replays), is what no
   Zombies-like has: dash pips, slides, wall rides, the ARCLINE, momentum as damage. Nothing may make it mushier: raw
   mouse input, the 120 Hz step, no acceleration, no animation lock. (Evidence: the movement shooters sit at 89-98 %
   positive; Sker Ritual's top negative is forced mouse acceleration: Research/2026-10-04-comparables.md 0.1.)
2. **Zombies rituals, ULTRAKILL rewards.** Rounds, gates, wall buys, perks, the Mystery Box, the power switch, THE
   FORGE, kill drops, a recurring special round, secrets and an exfil (CoD's loop) ... scored by style, freshness and
   parries (ULTRAKILL's): style pays points and survival, swapping guns keeps them fresh, a parry is offence.
   (ZOMBIES-ULTRAKILL-RESEARCH.md sections 1, 3.)
3. **Every run counts, and every run is readable.** The first minute teaches by doing; every screen reads at a glance
   (text >= 16 px at 1080p, a dusk palette where cyan is yours and red-orange is danger); RESULTS shows what you gained
   (records, contracts, mastery, the next unlock); one-press PLAY, at most three meta screens. (NEXT-WAVE-RESEARCH.md
   section 0; comparables 0.1, 0.9.)
4. **It runs well and plays anywhere.** Under 6.9 ms on the worst 1 % of frames on GG's laptop (Silent), no shader
   hitches, a pad from TITLE to RESULTS with its own glyphs, rebinding, save-and-quit at a break, Steam Deck ready text.
   Players review performance first in this genre. (NIGHT13-RESEARCH.md sections 0, 3, 4.)
5. **Grow by content, on proven ground.** New guns, enemies, rungs and maps arrive on a base that proves itself by
   machine (golden vectors, replays, the composed fingerprint, the smoke), so content nights are cheap and nothing old
   breaks. Only content cadence grows a game like this (ULTRAKILL's layers, Gunfire Reborn's seasons). (NIGHT13-RESEARCH
   section 0 item 8; comparables 0.2.)

## Not SKYGRIND (until GG says otherwise)

- Stat-stick grinding, battle passes, MTX (KF3's 44 % on Steam: comparables).
- Modes the data doesn't support before the demo: DUEL, CLASSIC ARENA, TRIALS, BOSS RUSH (D-21).
- Gore that stays (D-29: the dissolve), AI-made art, new packages before N17 (D-30).
- Online co-op before Early Access (D-02); co-op is tuned separately when it comes.
