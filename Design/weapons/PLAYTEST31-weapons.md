# FEEL-PLAYTEST31, the WEAPON section (draft, builder W; the lead folds it in)

1. **The order.** Level 1 gives you the KESTREL only. Level 2 gives you the PULSE gauntlet at the start of 2.1 TANK ALONE (walk over it and the pickup disappears). Level 3 gives you the THUNDERCLAP in 3.1 and level 4 the NAILGUN in 4.1. The weapon wheel and RB show only what you own. Say if any room asks for a gun you don't have yet.
2. **KESTREL, RT tap** (any room): it fires the moment you press, with no delay.
3. **KESTREL, RT hold** (1.8 PISTOL, at the three dummies in a line): hold about 0.9 s and the slot's CHARGING ring and the reticle ring fill. Let go and one shot pierces all three. Holding 0.5 s past full fires it for you. After that the slot reads COOLING for 4 s. Letting go early wastes nothing, and a melee press while charging cancels the charge. Say if the hold feels late.
4. **KESTREL, LT:** zoom x1.5. On the Lantern (1.12), only a charged shot fired from the wall breaks the shield (builder B's lantern31 rule; B confirms the wording).
5. **THUNDERCLAP, RT hold** (3.1, the last wave is a heavy): hold about 1.1 s for a SLUG, a single line instead of a spread, at 3x a full volley. It knocks husks back but never bosses, then COOLING 6 s. **LT:** zoom x1.25, with no shockwave.
6. **PULSE gauntlet, RT:** the slow, heavy punch. **LT before five kill pips:** a PARRY. It is only open for a quarter second, so press it the moment a Lantern orb, Stag shard or Wyrm spit is within arm's reach. The projectile flies back as a cyan streak and hurts the boss. Then the slot reads COOLING for 1 s.
7. **PULSE gauntlet, LT with five pips:** the slot reads BLAST, and LT blasts the cone in front of you and launches you (15 s cooldown). A press during the punch swing shows BUSY. Say if the parry window is too tight to hit on purpose.
8. **NAILGUN** (4.1): hold RT to stream. LT zooms while no nail is stuck in anything. Once nails stick, the slot reads DETONATE and LT sets them off.
9. **The combo** (4.1, once you own the NAILGUN and the KESTREL): stick nails in a body, then put a charged KESTREL shot through it. The nails go off at 1.5x.
10. **Watch for:** any LT press that does nothing with no word on the slot (there should be none), and any frame where a shot and a punch happen together.
