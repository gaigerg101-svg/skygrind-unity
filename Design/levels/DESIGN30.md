## A. THE WEAPON RULES (item 1; five sentences a player can read)
1. **You do one thing at a time:** a shot, a swing or a blast is an ACTION, and an action always finishes (its length is the weapon's attack time: a gun's shot cycle, the punch's 0.35 s swing).
2. **Switching is instant unless an action is running; then the switch happens the moment that action ends** (never later, never cancelled).
3. **A COOLDOWN is the wait after an action before that weapon can act again; it keeps running while the weapon is holstered**, so switching away never pauses it and switching back never restarts it.
4. **The PULSE gauntlet has two cooldowns of its own, the heavy swing (2.5 s) and the LT blast (15 s), and both follow rule 3**; the blast also needs five punch kills (the pips).
5. **Each weapon's slot on the HUD says its state in a word and a ring: READY (bright, full), COOLING (dim, draining, seconds left), CHARGING (a filling ring, for a charge shot or the pips).**

Diagnosis to confirm (S's first log line): Combat.cs:697 passes `CooldownOf(Weapon) > 0 || punchCooldown > 0` to `Lockout29.FilterSwitch`, so a switch waits for the WHOLE cooldown of the gun in hand (a 2.5 s swing cooldown, a slow gun's interval), while the gauntlet's heavy swing timer is not in that table; the result is "sometimes free, sometimes the full wait". The fix: the switch waits only for the ACTION (`min(cooldown left, actionSteps)`), the action length read from config (`design.n30.rules.actionSeconds` per class with a `_why`; the punch uses `punch.cycleTime` 0.35). One place: `Core/Guns/Rules30.cs`.
**Rejected alternative:** "a switch cancels the action" (ULTRAKILL-style animation cancel): rejected because GG's standing word is that fire and melee never overlap and the lockout stays; cancelling would let the punch and a shot land in the same 0.1 s.
