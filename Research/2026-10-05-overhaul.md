## 1. Kill crunch without sound (what ULTRAKILL, DOOM and the juice canon do)

**The sources:**
- ULTRAKILL wiki: Style https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Style, Feedbacker https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Feedbacker,
  Hitstop https://ultrakill.fandom.com/wiki/Hitstop, Movement https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Movement.
- Hakita's interview: https://80.lv/articles/ultrakill-devs-on-the-game-s-mechanics-npc-ai-and-early-access-experience
- DOOM Eternal: the combat Q&A https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-evolving-the-combat-design-of-id-software-s-i-doom-eternal-i-;
  destructible demons https://www.dsogaming.com/news/doom-eternal-tech-details-id-tech-7-can-display-10-times-higher-geometric-detail-demons-will-be-destructible/
- Sakurai on hitstop: https://sourcegaming.info/2015/11/11/thoughts-on-hitstop-sakurais-famitsu-column-vol-490-1/
- Street Fighter V hitstop: http://shoryuken.com/2016/06/07/hitstop-in-street-fighter-v-kens-not-so-little-secret/
- Vlambeer, "The Art of Screenshake": https://dkliao.itch.io/the-art-of-screenshake-recreation
- Eiserloh's trauma shake: https://github.com/IronWarrior/UnityCameraShake
- A study of Steam reviews on impact feedback: https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.06155

**The numbers:**
- ULTRAKILL [src]:
  - A major hitstop (the parry) freezes **0.25 s**; a minor one is shorter.
  - A parry is "a bright screen flash and a hitstop".
  - The style meter has 8 ranks with points from 200 to 1500. It decays at **15/s at the bottom up to 120/s at the top**,
    and taking damage drops you a rank.
  - The callouts are named lines: HEADSHOT 80, MULTIKILL 100 (within a **0.5 s** window), QUICKDRAW 50 (a hit within
    **0.5 s of a swap**), AIR SLAM 160.
  - Freshness runs from 1.5x down to 0x; movement multiplies style by up to 3x.
  - Every enemy bursts into meat, even on hits that don't kill.
  - Hakita: "the main focus was just on the game feel"; the retro look is there so "visuals are very easy to read at a
    glance"; the sound model is God Hand's "loud and raw".
- DOOM Eternal [src]:
  - Enemies stagger with a coloured glow, and every kind of kill pays out a resource you can see.
  - Hugo Martin made the enemies' wind-ups so fast "you can barely see them ... but they feel better".
  - Armour and flesh come off the body as it takes damage, so damage shows before the kill.
- Hitstop [src]:
  - Street Fighter V: 8 / 12 / 15 frames at 60 fps (133 / 200 / 250 ms).
  - Sakurai: more damage means a longer freeze, with a cap; attacker and victim freeze together; **the victim vibrates
    during the freeze**, its amplitude shrinking toward the end.
- Hit flash [src]: full white at once, eased back over about 0.1 s. An impact frame (one or two frames white or inverted)
  belongs to big moments only.
- Screen shake [src]: Eiserloh's trauma, from 0 to 1, decaying linearly. The shake is trauma² or trauma³ and drives
  **rotational** Perlin noise. For first person, about 1-3 deg of yaw / pitch and 2-4 deg of roll [rec]; never
  translate the camera.
- Vlambeer's list [src]: lower time-to-kill, a higher fire rate, bigger bullets, the muzzle flash, impact effects, enemy
  hit reactions and knockback, permanence, camera kick, the "sleep" freeze, and random explosions on a kill.
- The Steam-review study [src]: "hit stop, sound coherence and camera control" drive impact the most, and losing any one
  of them lowers it substantially.

**The ranking for SKYGRIND** (it already has hitstop tiers, FOV kicks, cube bursts, a hit marker, the fist and the
draws; the gap is **the victim's reaction and what's left behind**):
1. **Make the kill freeze read as intentional.** During the freeze the victim is fully white and vibrates (2-4 cm,
   decaying [rec]), and the burst spawns **on the freeze frame**. A freeze with nothing moving and no sound reads as lag:
   the biggest risk of a silent build.
2. **A hit flash on every damaging hit:** one frame of white, eased back over 0.1 s.
3. **The blocky model breaks into its own boxes on death:** each part flies along the shot with spin. They are boxes
   already, so this is nearly free.
4. **Rotational trauma shake** on kills and explosions (trauma²).
5. **A hit reaction on hits that don't kill:** the model tilts or pops away from the shot for 60-100 ms, view only.
6. **Style callouts** (+HEADSHOT, +MULTIKILL): each pops in at 1.3x and settles over about 120 ms, and they stack.
7. **A kill marker that is not the hit marker:** bigger, another shape, with a scale punch.
8. **Marks left behind:** decals in the enemies' red-orange. They clash with D-29 if they stay, so they fade.
9. **Viewmodel recoil as a spring impulse:** pitch up and back with roll, overshooting and settling, plus a camera kick.
10. **An impact frame** on parries and big multikills only.
11. **A bigger muzzle flash, on screen for one frame at most.**
12. **Faster swaps with a reward window** (QUICKDRAW 0.5 s): speed the draw in-tos up even if they look worse.

**Where sound can't be replaced:** "crunch" is mostly a transient sound, a bass thump plus a high crack on the impact
frame. Without audio, expect about 70 % of the feel [rec]. Mitigate it by putting the flash, the vibration, the shake
onset, the burst and the gameplay event **on the same frame**, so GG's sounds line up later.

## 2. UI, menus and identity

**The sources:**
- Xbox XAG 101: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101
- Steam Deck compatibility: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamdeck/compat
- Celia Hodent on onboarding: https://celiahodent.com/gamers-brain-ux-onboarding/
- DOOM Eternal's HUD: https://mspoweruser.com/doom-eternal-ui-explained-big-bold-loud/ and
  https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en/article/2V0erykuLGuV8a8GsmribP/doom-eternal-custom-huds
- Persona 5's UI: https://personacentral.com/persona-5-panel-concept-development-ui/
- Hi-Fi Rush's animation: https://www.gameanim.com/2023/09/08/hi-fi-rush-music-synced-animation/
- Progressive disclosure: https://ixdf.org/literature/topics/progressive-disclosure
- Pause menus: https://www.wayline.io/blog/20-reasons-your-pause-menu-is-sabotaging-your-game

**The rules [src, or rec where marked]:**
- **Text:**
  - XAG 101 sets PC text at ≥ 18 px at 1080p.
  - For the Steam Deck the smallest character must be at least 9 px at 1280x800 (12 px recommended). The CanvasScaler's
    800 / 1080 = 0.741 turns the 16 px floor into 11.9 px, so **raise the floor to 18 px** (13.3 px on the Deck).
  - Text lines are at most 80 characters; on the HUD, at most 3 words [rec].
- **Type scale [rec]:** a 1.333 ratio: 18 / 24 / 32 / 42 / 56 / 75 / 100 / 133. At most 4 sizes a screen and 2 font
  families.
- **Three tiers [rec]:**
  - glance (HP, ammo, points), readable in 200 ms: big numerals with no labels;
  - secondary (stamina, rank): an icon and a bar;
  - teaching (key hints): gone once learned.
  - DOOM Eternal's director: "big, bold and loud".
- **Onboarding (Hodent):**
  - At most 3 new things at once; teach by doing, in context, with a purpose; don't punish while teaching.
  - DOOM Eternal's pausing tutorial cards were widely called out.
  - For SKYGRIND: one verb per prompt ("[SHIFT] BOOST"), shown when it's useful, dismissed on the first use, shown again
    after about 60 s unused, never pausing the game.
- **The pause menu:** 4 items (RESUME / RESTART / SETTINGS / QUIT, with QUIT confirmed). CONTROLS and AIM ASSIST go into
  SETTINGS; COPY REPORT is a developer thing; a one-line run header on top.
- **Settings:**
  - 4-5 tabs: GAME / CONTROLS / VIDEO / AUDIO / ACCESSIBILITY.
  - At most 8 rows per tab before an "ADVANCED" fold.
  - Explanations only in a footer line for the focused row.
- **Case studies, what to steal:**
  - **ULTRAKILL:** the letter-plus-word rank slam, a decay bar under the letter, named lines with the newest on top, a
    rank drop on damage. But **build the rank ladder out of value and saturation inside cyan, not out of hues**:
    red-orange and magenta are taken (rule 8).
  - **CoD Zombies:** points pops that differ by kill quality; the round number as a ceremony.
  - **Persona 5:** one hero colour on near-black, text only in white or black, menus on one diagonal axis, the focused
    item lit and the rest dimmed.
  - **Neon White:** a large card-shaped weapon icon instead of a name and hint text.
  - **Hotline Miami:** the total's colour or heat predicts the grade.
- **Energetic score [rec]:**
  - **A pop:** scale 0 -> 1.3 -> 1.0 over 120 ms (ease-out-back), hold 350 ms, then rise 40 px and fade over 250 ms.
    Merge same-type pops within 300 ms into "+150 x3". Size by value: 24 / 32 / 42 px.
  - **The total:** an odometer or count-up over 250-600 ms (by the log of the delta, ease-out-cubic) with a punch of
    1.0 -> 1.15 -> 1.0 over 150 ms (at most once per 100 ms) and one white frame. **Monospaced digits.**
  - **The multiplier chip:** slams in from 1.6x with a 2-frame white flash and a thin drain bar under it.
  - **A rank-up:** the letter slams from 2x to 1x in 100 ms, the word shows for 600 ms, and the HUD shakes 4-6 px for
    100 ms. Only milestones shake.
  - **The end-of-round tally:** lines arrive 200 ms apart, each counting up over 300-500 ms; any input completes it.
  - **The round number:** 133-160 px at the centre for 1.5 s, then it tweens to its corner over 400 ms.
- **Identity [rec]:**
  - **A style tile** fixes: one display face, one body face, a numeral treatment, 3-5 core colours, a shape language, a
    motion language, and three sample components (the vitals block, a menu row, a score pop).
  - **Shape:** one slant (10-12 deg, matching the display italic), 45-degree corner cuts of 8-12 px, solid fills or
    3-4 px accent bars. **No 1 px outlines and no low-alpha grey plates**: translucent dark boxes, hairlines, low
    contrast and many sizes are what reads as cheap. Text gets a 2 px outline or a hard shadow.
  - **Motion:** in 120-180 ms ease-out-back with about 10 % overshoot, out 80-120 ms ease-in, nothing linear; menus enter
    along one diagonal.
  - **Fonts (OFL, Google Fonts):** Archivo (one variable family, widths 62-125, italics: Extra Condensed Black Italic for
    display, normal width for body), Tourney (for rank letters and the round number), Big Shoulders Display / Stencil,
    Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, Saira Extra Condensed, Anton, Kanit, Oxanium. Avoid Orbitron, Audiowide and Bebas Neue
    (synthwave cliches). Their pick: **Archivo + Tourney for the rank letters**.
- **The one-pass checklist [rec]**, for every screen. Each question should be answered yes:
  1. Is every line at most 8 words (3 on the HUD)?
  2. Has an icon or glyph replaced every word it could?
  3. Is text at least 18 px?
  4. Are there at most 4 sizes and at most 2 families?
  5. Is there one focal element, readable in 200 ms?
  6. Do the glance numbers have no labels?
  7. Are there no translucent grey plates and no 1 px borders?
  8. Is there one slant and one corner cut?
  9. Does every colour keep its role?
  10. Are digits monospaced?
  11. Does every number change animate?
  12. Is every entry 180 ms or less, ease-out, and nothing linear?
  13. Is shake kept to milestones?
  14. Does the pause menu have at most 5 items?
  15. Does each settings tab have at most 8 rows before ADVANCED?
  16. Are explanations only in the focus footer?
  17. Does each prompt teach one verb, in context, and go away on use?
  18. Are there at most 3 new things on screen while the player learns?
  19. Do the glyphs match the device?
  20. Is everything inside a 48-64 px inset?
  21. Does the screen survive greyscale?
  22. Does it read at 50 % size?
  23. Is nothing shown that the player can't act on?
  24. Would you post this frame on Steam?

## 3. One bar, a sprint and a partial boost

**The sources:**
- Vanquish: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/dissecting-the-core-mechanics-of-vanquish
- Halo's sprint and abilities: https://www.halopedia.org/Sprint and https://www.halopedia.org/Spartan_abilities
- ULTRAKILL's movement: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Movement
- DOOM's dash: https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Dash
- Warframe's stamina: https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Stamina
- Dying Light 2 removing parkour stamina: https://www.resetera.com/threads/dying-light-2s-newest-update-removes-stamina-from-the-parkour-system-entirely-now-tied-exclusively-to-combat-instead.1273788/
- Players on limited sprint: https://www.resetera.com/threads/games-need-to-stop-limiting-sprinting.429155/
- CoD's Tactical Sprint: https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-10/The-Basics-of-Call-of-Duty-Modern-Warfare-Movement

**What other games do [src]:**
- **Vanquish:** one gauge pays for the boost-slide and the slow-mo; emptying it overheats it and locks you out.
- **Halo:** Reach's sprint lasted 5 s with a 2.5 s cooldown; Infinite's sprint is only about 8 % faster than walking.
- **ULTRAKILL:** 3 stamina; a dash costs 1, a dash-jump 2; it refills at 0.7/s and not while sliding; a parry refills
  it all.
- **DOOM Eternal:** 2 dash charges, which refill only on landing.
- **Warframe and Dying Light 2** removed stamina from movement: Warframe in 2015, Dying Light 2 by a patch that kept it
  for combat only.
- **Titanfall and Apex:** unlimited sprint.
- **Mirror's Edge Catalyst:** motion fills a shield.
- **Ghostrunner and Splitgate:** a small meter under the crosshair.

**The lesson:** movement shooters keep basic motion free. A sprint that drains a combat resource makes players walk
("dropping to normal speed feels like moving backwards"). GG's design can work if the sprint is cheap, the bar refills
fast, and **the slide stays the fastest free move**.

**Tap versus hold on one key:** you only know a press was a tap when the key comes up, so a hold-to-sprint on the boost
key would add about 150-200 ms to every boost (estimated, not measured). Two ways out:
- keep the boost on the press, and let a hold continue as a sprint (Vanquish);
- or give the sprint its own key.

**The recommended numbers [rec]:**
- **The bar:** 1.0 pays for one full boost.
- **The boost** (fires on the press, at 24 m/s as now):
  - It **scales its duration with the fill**, not its speed (a slower dash feels mushy), so the distance scales
    linearly.
  - A floor of 0.25 (8 steps); a fill of 0.9 or more counts as full, so nobody waits for the last pixel.
  - The invulnerability frames scale with the duration.
- **The sprint:** 10.5 m/s (under the slide's 11.6), draining 0.5 bar/s, ending by itself with no lockout.
- **The refill:** 0.8 bar/s, 0.4 s after the last spend (0.8 s if the sprint emptied the bar).
- **Softening one bar:** refill on a kill, a slam landing or a parry (ULTRAKILL's parry, DOOM's glory kill); make the
  second bar an early upgrade (VIGOR).
- **The HUD:** a thin bar under the crosshair, dim and hollow below the boost floor, solid cyan above it, one flash when
  full, and an event (`staminaFull`) for GG's sound.

## 4. The port today (the code audit)

**Stamina and dash:**
- `Mover.pips` (Mover.cs:131) and `MaxPips = dash.maxPips + bonusPips` (:232). The refill runs at
  `dash.regenPerSec x regenMult` (:353), and not while dashing (MovementMath.cs:158-162).
- The config: `dash` = speed 24, duration 0.25, pipCost 1, maxPips 3, regenPerSec 0.7; `dashJump.extraPipCost` 1; VIGOR
  +1 pip.
- `TryDash` (Mover.cs:632-662) sets the speed to max(entry, 24), runs for `StepsFor(0.25)` and is **invulnerable for the
  whole dash** (:659).
- **There is no sprint anywhere.**
- `InputFrame.dash` already carries `held` (Mover.cs:32), and the tape packs held and pressed per button
  (RunTape.cs:76). **A sprint needs no change to the input or the tape steps.**
- The pad's dash is LB.

**How to add `design.stamina`:**
- Copy NIGHT16's `design.move` pattern: a pure `Core/Stamina.cs` with Params / Neutral / Any / State; one Mover field;
  hooks guarded by `rules.Any`; `SurvivalSim.Stamina.cs` for the flags; tape format 3 with a stamina flags byte (formats
  1-2 still replay with it off); "stamina" in `MoverState.Fields`, optional for old saves; the golden vectors never
  call it.
- **Risks:**
  - The bot: `BotBrain.cs:399` and `Nav/Follower.cs:265/277`'s dash-gap links assume a full-speed dash, so require a full
    bar for those links and run the farm.
  - The dash-jump's extra cost in bar units.
  - `RestoreStamina` (Combat.cs:1054/1116).
  - The smoke asserts `MaxPips == dash.maxPips + 1` (SliceSmoke.cs:133).
  - Both HUD views are pip-based (Ui.cs:313-322 / 1266-1277; UiFeelHud.cs:329-345).
  - The sprint has no FOV, speed-line or tint cue (CameraFeel.cs:121, UiFeel.cs:100, MovementFx.cs:336 and PostFx.cs:77
    all key on `MoveState.Dash`).
  - The labels say DASH.
  - The fingerprint will move, since `SimFingerprint.cs:203` dashes every 420 steps.

**The UI:**
- 34 Ui partial files, **9,145 lines**: Ui.cs 1,585, UiMeta 744, UiFeelHud 603, UiControls 538, UiNav 497, UiHotbar 478,
  UiResults and UiOnboarding 372 each. Also 28 UnityTuning files, 2,014 lines, and about 55 `new Color(` literals.
- **PAUSED** has 8 buttons (Ui.cs:424-438), 7 shown at once, beside a full YOUR KEYS card.
- **TITLE:** the run setup row (SURVIVAL / DAILY, MAP, HEAT) and 7 buttons, a controls line of about 190 characters, and
  a version stamp.
- **SETTINGS:** **50 rows** in 7 tabs (MOUSE+AIM 5, VIDEO 8, HUD 6, ACCESSIBILITY 10, GAME 3, GAMEPAD 9, LAB 9).
- **The wordiest strings:** the STALE tip (113 characters), the slow-mo row's text (108), the LAB note (101), the display
  mode text (99), the lessons ("{dash} DASHES, {slide} SLIDES (SLAMS IN THE AIR): STYLISH MOVES RAISE YOUR POINTS").
- **Fonts:** Barlow Semi Condensed 600 / 700 and Chakra Petch 600 / 700 italic, as static SDF atlases
  (Editor/ThemeFonts.cs). CLAUDE.md rule 8's "TMP default until converted" is stale.
- **HUD sizes:** the hit marker 8, the edge margin 22, the crosshair ticks 10x4; type 16 / 17 / 20 / 34 / 44 / 52.

**The effects:**
- **A death today:** 26 cubes of 0.12 m at 7 m/s living 0.6 s (pool 400); the corpse frozen white and vibrating, then
  dissolving over 0.22 s (32 corpse slots); a kill-confirm ring; a light pop; a 0.04-0.09 s freeze; trauma.
- **A hit today:** 5 cubes, a white flash, a 0.05 m render flinch.
- **Hooks:** `EnemyLook.Die` (EnemyViews.cs:324) already snapshots each part's matrix, mesh and colour, so each part can
  get a velocity and spin, view only. A splatter would be a Decals.cs variant on `kill`. Hit direction = the position
  minus `game.Eye`. `kill` and `hit` fire inside the sim step, so handlers only queue. Allocation rules: PORT-RULES 6.
- **D-29 (GG's: "no gore that stays, the dissolve")** constrains gibs and splatter; anything that stays needs GG to
  reverse it.

**The tools:**
- `-skygrindStrip <dir> [scenario]`: freeze, punch, punch-press, parry, slowmo, slide, land, swap, draw, plus `results`
  when named; **there is no plain-kill or horde scenario yet**.
- `-skygrindShots <dir> [-skygrindShotsUi]`: TITLE, the GAMEPAD tab, the map views, ACCESS, LAB, PAUSED, DOWN, RESULTS.
- A Player build takes 20-42 s; one build's full checks take about 7 min; the smoke alone 3-7 min.
