## 1. The one verb: the LAUNCH (VERBS.md row 41)
A punch thrown while dashing, sliding or wall riding launches the husk about 12 m. A launched husk that hits another husk or a wall bursts:
60 damage in 3.5 m (design.n36.launch36 speed 16 x flightS 0.8, burstDamage 60, burstRadius 3.5).

- **What the room forces:** level 1's biggest fight (11 enemies) comes at you as PACKS through two 3 m GATES in a 1.8 m slag wall, and once
  along the gantry over them. A pack bunched in a 3 m gate is the launch's target: dash in and punch the front husk back into the pack. The
  burst kills every husk within 3.5 m (husk.hp 40) and every spitter (spitter.hp 60).
- **Why it is the answer (numbers from the config):**
  - 4 husks = 8 KESTREL body shots (25 damage each, 0.4 s apart) = 3.2 s.
  - The pack closes from the gate (12-14 m) at husk.speed 6 m/s in about 2 s.
  - So shooting alone gives ground; one launch clears the gate.
  - The gate posts and the anvil are walls a launched husk bursts on (launch36 wallProbe), so a punch near a gate pays even with one husk.
  - It is level 1's exam: it uses the dash (taught in a1), the ride (a3) and the punch / parry (a4) together. Wave 2's spitters on the gantry
    lob over the gates, so a4's parry pays here too.
- **Why it cannot be exploited:**
  - Standing at a gate's mouth gets you surrounded: the gates are 3 m, the pack pushes through, and husk.meleeRange is 2.2 m.
  - Wave 2's spitters stand 4 m up over the gates, so a still player at a gate is under lobs.
  - The gantry is not a camp: the husks walk up its stair, and wave 3 comes along it.
  - **Honest: "the launch cannot be skipped" is not checked.** The walk bot shoots its way through, and n46flow prints no launch count. The
    launch line is framed by tour65's line65; verbs37 row 41 (launch) is the lead's Player run.
- **GG:** "wide open arenas with a roof ... I prefer that side of game play" (2026-10-13); "they can't be too exploitable"; "flying through
  and ... killing the enemies ... in a stylish fashionable way"; GG asked for explosions (w36, launch36's _why). Kept from his 2026-10-12
  ban: no lava, no pit, no pillar without a job.
