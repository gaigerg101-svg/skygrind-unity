## The one verb, and why
**The slide-launch: slide under THE TAP BEAM (a 1.2 m slot through THE TAP WALL), come out onto THE APRON at slide speed among the husks that
hold the pour's lip, and punch: a punch thrown while sliding launches (launch36: 16 m/s x 0.8 s, lift 5 m/s); THE POUR is 4 m ahead, 7 m deep.**
- **Is a slide-only gap possible in this sim? Yes (read):** Design/levels/LEVEL-FORMAT.md "Tunnels": `{zone, rect, ceiling}` makes a ceiling
  slab from `ceiling` up 2 m; "With `ceiling` 1.2 m: the player must slide (slide collider 1.0 m), a swarmer (0.75 m) walks through, a husk
  (1.8 m) goes round. The report runs the real Mover through it sliding (passes) and running (stopped)" (validator row 7). PortExport/config.json:
  body.capsuleHeight 1.8, slide.colliderHeight 1.0. So the slot is proved slide-only by the validator itself, every levelgen run.
- GG 2026-10-12: "random lava pits ... that serve no purpose"; 2026-10-13: "wide open arenas with a roof"; "killing the enemies ... in a stylish
  fashionable way". The pour stays only as the slide-launch's target: a body in the lava dies (design.n30.lava), the player is burned and knocked
  up (knockUp 9) onto walkable floor. D-3: no other card forces the slide; b1 forces the launch from the ride, so a5's launch comes from the slide.
- **D-3, the honest version (the lead's ruling at the re-gate, 1:11):** D-3 as first written ("the way to the pour's lip passes under a low
  beam only a slide clears") **cannot be met** without making THE APRON a pocket. The slot has no walk node (ai.grid.minClear 1.9 > its 1.2 m
  ceiling), so closing THE TAP WALL's ends would leave wave 2's husks and the nav with no way out. What the room does instead:
  - THE TAP WALL (x -7..2) closes the middle. **The slide slot is the short, direct line to the lip:** from the south floor's middle you slide
    through it and come out heading north at the pour at slide speed (11.6 m/s), with wave 2's husks in front, between you and the lava.
  - **The walk round the west end is the long way.** Wave 2's two apron husks now stand east of the slot's line, at (-0.5, -131.5) and
    (1, -131): 6.5 and 8 m from the apron's west end (x -7). The card's earlier "6-9 m" was wrong: the old (-5, -131) node was 2 m from it.
  - **The east end (x 2) is not long:** the husks are 1-2.5 m from it, so a walk round the tap block's east end and a dash-punch from the side
    skips the slide too. Round 3's candidate: carry THE TAP BLOCK east to the rail's lane.
  - The launch itself is "a punch thrown while dashing, sliding, wall riding or grapple-pulled" (design.n36.launch36._why): a dash-punch from
    either end launches as well. The slide is the room's offered line, not a forced one.
- **Stopping room:** the apron is 4 m deep (z -129..-133); a released slide stops in about 2 m by ground friction 6 (my arithmetic, not run).
  A slide held into the lava is a burn and a knock-up out (fair: Lava30), not a death.
- **Not exploitable / cannot be skipped:** the KESTREL kills everything too, so the launch is rewarded (the kills, style), not forced: "the verb
  cannot be skipped": not checked. The slot itself is checked every build (validator row 7). **Not checked:** where a launched husk lands
  (5-12 m by the numbers; the pour is 7 m deep in z ahead of the slot).
