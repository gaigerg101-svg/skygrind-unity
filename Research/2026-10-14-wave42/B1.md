## The attacks
- SWEEP (close; a RED 0.9 s tell, the arm cocked low, a ring on the ice at its 4 m reach): one spin at shin height, starting on the far
  side (it reaches you 0.2 s after the tell). Answer: jump (or dash out of reach). Dizzy 0.7 s after: the punish. Read: high fist = dash
  (haymaker), low arm = jump (sweep) (Sekiro's sweep = jump, research/boss-web.md).
- LEAP (past 7 m; RED 1.1 s tell): a landing circle tracks you for 60 %, then locks; it jumps and lands: a hit inside 3.2 m; its fists
  stuck 1.0 s (the punish). Answer: dash / run out of the locked circle. Phase 2: the landing sends the slam's ring too.
- Phase 2: rests 0.8 -> 0.55 s; a haymaker chains into a sweep (0.6) when the slam chain did not fire.
