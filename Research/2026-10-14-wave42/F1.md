# F1 hits and kills (NIGHT42; G9, G15): notes

## The rows
- impact42 (sim bit; Core/ImpactRules.cs + Game/SurvivalSim.N42.Impact.cs): freeze lengths. Numbers in design.n42.impact42, each with a _why
  from research/hitfeel-web.md.
- kills42 (view row; Game/KillFeel42.cs): the gauntlet's ring, kick, sparks; the kill's debris and trauma; the chunks' harder throw. It draws
  impact42's events, so the two rows go on together.
