## What each row does

- meter42 (UiHud38.Meter42.cs): the outer band is the special (a charge fills with the true level, cyan only at a real FULL, a cooldown
  refills in dim bone, the empty track = ready to charge, a ping at a cooldown's end); the inner lines are cooldowns only (line 1 the BLAST,
  line 2 the normal attack: the PULSE's swing, the THUNDERCLAP's pump). The swing is read from the gauntlet's own cooldown
  (Loadout.CooldownStepsOf("gauntlet")): W1's gauntlet42 shows as it is if it keeps the swing there.
- style42 (UiHud33.Style42.cs): the sim's six ranks (D C B A S SS, DULL .. SKYGRIND); no SSS (a rank is sim state: a view row cannot add
  one). The letter punches, shakes, drops, dims as the band drains, breathes at the top, turns red-orange on a hit; the combo count, the
  rank's name, the last award.
- health42 (UiHud33.Health42.cs): the damage chunk (held 0.4 s, then drained), the number counting, the punch and shake on a hit, the
  heal's cyan glow (first to cut), the low pulse.
