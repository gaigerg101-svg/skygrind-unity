# F4: does a KESTREL tap pressed and released inside a freeze fire? (a check; nothing in the game changes)

- **The finding, plainly:** usually yes, late (after the freeze and the gun's 0.48 s interval); but when the shot that started the freeze fired on its own press step (the KESTREL while its charge cools, 2.5 s after every charged shot), the tap is lost.
- **Why (code read):** both readers latch a press and read held live (CombatReader.cs:130-133, PadReader.cs:392), so the step after the freeze sees `pressed, !held`: p39's "click inside one step" (Charge35.cs:546-554). But `pressed = input.fire.pressed && !q.pressedLast` (Charge35.cs:500, the n35-r3 rule) merges it with the step before, and a freeze cuts the frame's step loop right after the step that started it (SurvivalGame.cs:333-338): when that step was pressed, the tap's press reads as the same press.
- **Measured headless (sim side, taps71h.txt):** taps inside freezes 6, fired 3: release-tap freezes 3 / 3; press-step freezes 0 / 3 (p39 press taps +0); the control with one step between 3 / 3.
- **The Player line (the lead):** `bash Tools/night/n71-reels.sh <tag> tp,n52-foundry,taps71` prints `[reel] taps71: <GREEN|RED> taps71 | taps inside freezes N, fired M | release-tap kills ... | press-step kills ...`, each tap with its kill step's press bit. Expected from the probe: RED, M < N, the losses all press-step.
- **Off flag:** none (a check). Headless twin: `dotnet run --project Tools/SimHarness -c Release -- taps71h [--level n52-foundry] [--reps 3]`.
- **The fix (not built: LEFTOVER L10 press71):** Game-side, no Core change: when a freeze cut the loop after a pressed step and a fresh press was latched in the freeze, the first step after it samples the press as not yet pressed, and the next step as pressed (8.3 ms later). Golden and fingerprints would not move (headless runs have no freeze); the tape records what the sim saw.
- **Where it bites in play:** the KESTREL inside 2.5 s of a charged shot; the THUNDERCLAP's air tap (tap36 fires on the press: not measured here); any one-step click.
