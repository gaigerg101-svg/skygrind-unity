1. WHAT THE SHIPPED STREAM IS (read from JumpPadView27.MakeStream, Pad27.cs, SurvivalGame.Init.cs:317-331)
   The stream is a ParticleSystem on the pad's glow material: a copy of the neon material, URP Unlit, additive (One / One), _BaseColor = colors.usable x world.neonBase (1.25): HDR cyan.
   URP Unlit ignores particle (vertex) colours and the quads have no texture, so every mote is a HARD-EDGED FLAT SQUARE of full cyan: 0.13 m at its biggest, 0.026 m at birth, shrinking to
   nothing at death, 12 a second, 1.5 s, 2 m/s. At 15-30 m that is 1-4 px: a quad that small gains and loses pixel centres as the camera turns. The n74-t1 b1 mid shot
   (Logs/feel/n74-t1/pf/pan74/shot-N74-pan74-n65-foundry-b1-mid.png) shows them: two hard cyan squares of 15-20 px beside the beam at about 5 m. The URP assets say m_MSAA: 1 (no MSAA).

2. WHAT pads74 DOES (Pad74.cs, Pad74Math.cs, JumpPadView27.cs; numbers UnityTuning.N74L.cs N74Pad*)
   Every pad gets a second ParticleSystem, the steady twin; JumpPadView27.Update calls Pad74View.Apply(NightRows.ViewOn(cfg, "pads74")) every frame: one renderer on, the other off. Both
   simulate always (a disabled renderer does not stop a ParticleSystem) and both get the launch burst. The row off draws exactly the shipped stream.
   The twin: 5 motes a second (not 12), 0.34 m (not 0.13), 1.5 m/s for 2 s (the same 3 m of rise, a quarter slower), a SOFT sprite (a sheet of 32 frames of one blob, (1 - r^2)^2, exactly 0 at the
   quad's edge; brightness per frame fades in over 0.30 of the life and out over the last 0.45), a size curve that never goes under 0.6 of its peak (0.2 m: 4.4 px at 25 m), tint 0.7, a fixed
   random seed per pad (the shipped stream's is random: two OFF runs differ in the pad blocks, two ON runs draw the same motes).
   The 16-frame first draft jumped 57 % in one brightness step; the pure test caught it; the sheet is 32 frames.
