## The old calibration and why (n74-t2, python -I -B Tools/night/n74-warp-quality.py Logs/feel/n74-t2/pp2/pan74; flick74-short.txt has the table)
It picked x0.92 (vertical fov 68.1 deg) because its energy included the gun and the HUD; the real shift of the sweeps (a brute-force registration of each sweep's first and middle frame, 90 frames apart) is 2.56 - 2.60 px a frame at the centre, i.e. fov about 72.8 (x0.984).
Over the world core the old warp left 0.37 - 0.44 of the plain difference (v2 / v5 / v6); a calibration at the real fov should leave less.
