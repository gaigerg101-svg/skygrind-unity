n56-room.py in_wedge: a wedge's yaw is RADIANS (NIGHT74 builder L, 2026-10-28 00:5x; tree n74-L at 8a8c13e21 + this commit's files, uncommitted when written). S's finding (Research/2026-10-27-wave74/S/ledger.md, in S's tree).

## The defect
Tools/night/n56-room.py in_wedge did `yaw = math.radians(w.get('yaw', 0))`. The level files' collision wedges carry the yaw in radians: the values in every shipped level are 0, +-1.5708 and 3.1416 (python over Design/levels/n52-{foundry,iceline,kiln,span,spire}.json),
and the sim's collider reads it as radians (PortData.Wedge -> WedgeSpec.yaw -> ArenaMap.WedgeCorners: JsMath.Sin(w.yaw)). So a ramp turned a quarter or half turn stood where a yaw-0 ramp would (1.5708 deg = 0.027 rad): ray_clear, IN VIEW's seen counts, the beside rule and the
"line into the entry corridor" clause were wrong wherever a rotated ramp stands between an eye and a node. Fix: wedge_yaw() returns the yaw as stored; `--legacy-wedge-degrees` keeps the old reading for the tables below. No other file reads a wedge's yaw (n57-l-room.py and n59-l-room.py only load the list and call this ray_clear).

## The planted case (python -I -B Tools/night/n56-room.py wedge-planted [--legacy-wedge-degrees])
A 6 x 4 m ramp rising 3 m along its yaw from (0, 0); rays at 1.6 m; across the high end (2.5 m tall there) the ray must be BLOCKED, over the low end (0.5 m) CLEAR, for yaw 0, +pi/2, -pi/2 and pi.
fixed:  8 of 8 hold, exit 0 ("every rotated wedge blocks and clears as the sim collider would")
legacy: yaw 0 holds both; +pi/2 and -pi/2 FAIL both (the high-end ray reads clear, the low-end one blocked: the ramp stands where a yaw-0 one would); pi FAILs its high-end case (5 of 8 FAIL), exit 1.
The existing `planted` command is unaffected by the fix (every check still FAILs its planted case: "trusted", fixed and legacy).
