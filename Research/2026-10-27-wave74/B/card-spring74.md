## Why spring72's squash could not be seen (read from the code, simulated, not measured in a Player)
- spring72 kicks its flinch spring with a VELOCITY (Spring72FlinchKick -15.5): the squash grows over the frames after the hit and peaks (< 17 %, capped by Spring72MaxSquash 0.2) about five frames on.
- crunch36's own pop (Crunch36Squash 0.15, white hold 0.04-0.07 s, knock, twist, stagger tip) is at its peak ON the hit frame. On the frames a close crop looks at (hit, hit + 1, hit + 2) the old pop was all there was.

## What the row does
- A hit (the sim's health falling by >= 6 % of the body's maximum since the frame before it was drawn, >= 0.08 s after the last kick: a burn tick or one pellet of a burst is not a hit) SETS a second spring's displacement to -0.32 of the height (a graze 0.6 of that, up to the full 0.32 at a 25 % hit) on the frame the hit is drawn, and HOLDS it 0.03 s of frame time: the hit frame and the two after it (the strip's hit + 1, + 2) at full depth. Then a 9 Hz, z 0.4 spring lets it go through a stretch of about 7 % and is quiet by 0.2 s (simulated at 60 fps: -0.32 x3 frames, then -0.15, +0.02, +0.07, +0.04, 0.00).
- Volume-preserving (y by 1 + s, x and z by 1 / sqrt(1 + s)), on the feet, capped at +-0.42, not drawn under REDUCED MOTION or EFFECTS 0, multiplied with crunch36's, hitzone47's and spring72's: the body is about 0.85 x 0.68 = 58 % as tall and a third wider for three frames, the same pill squashed (rule 7: motion is mine).
- Where: Game/EnemyViews.Spring74.cs (the row, Spring74Math, Spring74Probe, the hold and the spring), hooked by one line in Spring72React (EnemyViews.Spring72.cs, before its `if (!draw) return;`: spring74 draws whether spring72's row is on or off) and four fields on its S72 state. Numbers: UnityTuning.N74B.cs (each with its reason). Tests: N74BSpringTests (Kick thresholds and scale, the curve at 60 fps: held through hit + 2, released, stretch inside the cap, volume 1; the numbers' reasons: squash >= 2x crunch36's).
