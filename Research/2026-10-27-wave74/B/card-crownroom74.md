## Where the violet comes from (read from code and config, not measured in a Player)
- colour56 gives the spire a slate wall ramp (wallBaseHex 0x1d1f24 -> wallUpperHex 0x6e7280, hue 225-227, S 0.12-0.18).
- palette72 multiplies the stone's wall class (walls AND undersides) by x(1.23, 0.93, 2.92) (UnityTuning.Palette72WallMul, calibrated on the spire's amber-lit rooms; the Player log: `[palette72] spire ON: ... wall x(1.23, 0.93, 2.92) ... 2636 over 1.0`). On the upper slate that is (0.53, 0.42, 1.46): blue past 1.0 = violet. In the boss room (cyan pool lights, lavender ambient, no amber) nothing balances it.
- The olive ceiling grid is the same wall class (an underside) under the ground-colour ambient. The stone's fog starts at 45 m and these walls look 10-30 m away, so it is not the sky.
- Brown = the crown's bronze blades (its identity, kept). Red = the tells (danger, kept). Cyan = the ring (weak74), the pools and the piece edges (player-usable, kept). Rose / pink block = a piece's danger tell glow (kept).
