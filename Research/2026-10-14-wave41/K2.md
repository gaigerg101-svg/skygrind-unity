## Round 1: the room light's colour by theme

**The root cause (read in code):** LightPlan33.Green names every room light "warm" (the key panel :372-373, an open room's flood :361-366, the
sconces :417-423, the pool panels :436-437); LightKit29.Named maps "warm" to UnityTuning.Look33PanelWarmHex 0xffe6c8 for every level, and
CampaignLighting24.Update sets the key spot to the same hex (:201). The theme (LightPlan33.Theme, from the map's name) fed only the post,
fog and ambient. So the iceline tint from NIGHT40 (L2) was multiplied by a warm light: ~0.9 / 0.7 / 0.5 of R / G / B (NIGHT40-LOG L2).

**What changed (view only; no sim file touched):**
- `Assets/Scripts/Game/UnityTuning.Look41.cs`: a key and a fill hex per theme (order foundry, iceline, kiln, span, spire), a comment each;
  the reserved-colour rule (hue gap >= 18 deg or saturation < 0.15).
- `Assets/Scripts/Game/Look41.cs`: `ThemeFor(cfg, json)` = `LightPlan33.Theme(json)` while `N41View.RowOn(cfg, "look41")`, else -1;
  `KeyHex(theme)` / `FillHex(theme)` (-1 = NIGHT40's exact hexes); `HueSat`, `ClearOf`.
- `LightKit29`: `Named(cfg, name, theme41)` (the theme argument); `theme41` read once at Build; a new `ColorNamed(cfg, name, theme41)`
  overload (the old one keeps -1, so PropsView29's colours are unchanged).
- `CampaignLighting24`: `theme41` read once at Build; the key spot's colour, Colour33 (the fixture meshes' "warm" panels and lenses) and
  the lights35 fill take the theme's key / fill.
- The level's theme at light-build time: the map JSON's `name` (n41 placeholders keep their levelFrom's theme word: n41-kiln -> kiln).

**Round 2c (the lead's leak check, read in code):**
- **a58c9049 would have drawn nothing:** SurvivalGame.Init (SurvivalGame.Init.cs:121-131) builds CampaignLighting24 BEFORE it destroys every
  directional light and Look.Apply makes the sun from colors.lightSun, so a tint written at build lands on no sun (first load) and is
  replaced at once. Now the tint is written in CampaignLighting24.Update (Sun41Tick), only when it differs.
- **Unload paths:** a map change, the level select, the menu and survival all reload the scene (SceneManager.LoadScene in
  SurvivalGame.Maps.cs:84 / 131 / 147, SurvivalGame.Campaign25.cs:43, UiLevelSelect26.cs:320), and Look.Apply then makes a fresh config sun:
  nothing carries over. A respawn keeps the level (the tint stays, correctly). OnDestroy also puts the config colour back if ours is
  still on the sun.
- **Boss rooms keep the config's warm sun** (RoomDef.kind "boss": x9 THE ICEBREAKER, k5, o3, v11, a9). The tells themselves are unlit
  (Brawl40View's glow / decal / line / floe tints on the additive neon; TelegraphView on the Field shader; Enemy.shader reads no main light),
  but additive tells lie over the floor: the slam decal adds 0.9 x 0xff3a14 = (230, 52, 18). Over today's tan floor (150, 130, 119) that
  gives about (255, 182, 137), red-orange (hue 22). Over an iceline-sun floor of about (115, 125, 165) it would be about (255, 177, 183),
  hue 356, a pink. That is an estimate, not checked on a frame, so the boss rooms keep the light their tells were tuned under.

## Round 2: level 2's canal (frost and ice sheen as view props)

`Assets/Scripts/Game/Ice41View.cs`, built from CampaignLighting24.Build next to SkateFx27; nothing is built unless look41 is on and the map's
theme is iceline (Look41.ThemeFor == 1). No level data edited; NIGHT40's stone tint (UnityTuning.Ice40*) unchanged; no collider; a hash per
piece (the same every run, no RNG). Numbers in UnityTuning.Look41.cs (Ice41*).
- **Frost:** a ragged skirt round the base of every wall box >= 1.5 m tall: pieces 1.1 m long, 0.18-0.7 m high, 0.03 m proud, 80 % of
  them drawn, colour 0xd4e4f4 on the lit stone material (it takes the room's light). It starts at the highest floor top touching the wall (a
  wall sunk into the floor). The cyan ride stripes stay clear: the lower stripe is at 1.6 m, the end caps stand 0.04 m out (frost 0.03).
- **Sheen:** additive streaks (0xc8dcff x 0.10) along each floor box's long side, one per 4 m of its short side (max 6), 0.3-1.1 m wide,
  35-80 % of the long side.
- **The canal (campaign.surfaces kind "ice", g4's channel):** a whole glaze over the ice rect (0xbcd4ff x 0.07) and streaks at x 0.16.

Counted on Design/levels/n40-iceline.json with a node mirror of the rules: 85 walls -> 2654 pieces before the 80 % share (~2120 drawn),
23 floor boxes -> 72 streaks, 1 glaze. Not checked: any frame.

**Prediction for the frames:** in g1 the wall bases show a pale blue-white crust (brighter than the wall above it under the same light), and
the floor shows faint lighter streaks running down the room's long way; in g4 the canal's floor reads lighter and bluer than the floors
beside it. The mean floor colour gains a little more blue from the sheen (additive 0.10 of c8dcff on ~25 % of the floor: about +3 / +4 / +5
of 255 in R / G / B where it lies).
