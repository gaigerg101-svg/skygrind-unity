# FORGE70 design research: make the verb the faster clear (LAUNCH into pits, RIDE that pays)

2026-10-25, web research only (no game code read). Tags on every finding: **[read]** = the page was fetched and read;
**[summary]** = seen only in a search-result summary of that page (page not opened, or the fetch was blocked); treat as weaker.
GDC Vault talks were members-only: only their public overviews were read.

## 1. Knockback into a hazard

- **Bulletstorm names our exact problem.** Postmortem: "A headshot is a much faster kill than leashing an enemy toward you, then kicking him into a cactus." The team's answer was to pay for it: "Hey, why don't we actually reward people for being creative?" -> the Skillshot system; in PvP "you could win with fewer kills than the opposite team". The kick is one button whose "purpose can change": kick away, **break armour**, open doors. Hazards are movable ("explosive trash cans ... can be moved around"; "kick an enemy into a trash can, which then explodes"), and chain reactions are designed for. [read] https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/classic-postmortem-people-can-fly-s-i-bulletstorm-i-
- Bulletstorm: points for kicking enemies onto spikes (secondary source). [summary] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletstorm ; Chmielarz lists kick, slide, leash, weapons and interactive objects as the core verbs; kick into an explosive barrel. [summary] https://gamingbolt.com/bulletstorm-an-exclusive-interview-with-adrian-chmielarz-creative-director-of-people-can-fly
- **ULTRAKILL prices the hazard kill and the enemy-on-enemy kill.** Style bonuses: + FALL 80 (enemy into a bottomless pit), + OUT OF BOUNDS 80, + SPLATTERED 100 (fall damage), + FRIENDLY FIRE 200 hit / 250 kill (one enemy hits another), + PARRY 100, + PROJECTILE BOOST 90. Hitting another body is worth MORE than the pit: the burst is a reward there, not a failure. Style rank also speeds Hard Damage recovery (ULTRAKILL rank = instant), so style is survival, not only score. [read] https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Style ; hazards that kill enemies: [summary] https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Pit
- ULTRAKILL's AI is "purposefully simple" so enemies are "predictable and easy to keep track of"; the stated problem is making enemies that threaten a player this mobile. [summary] https://80.lv/articles/ultrakill-devs-on-the-game-s-mechanics-npc-ai-and-early-access-experience/
- **Dark Messiah** (the kick-into-spikes reference): the kick "pushes them into environmental hazards, such as bottomless pits or jagged beds of spikes" (hands-on preview); a review notes levels sprawl vertically so enemies often stand near a drop. [summary] https://gamebanshee.com/rd4x
- **Sifu**: players "cleverly utilise windows and ledges" (reveal article) [summary] https://checkpointgaming.net/news/2021/02/sifu-is-the-new-stylish-martial-arts-game-by-sloclap/ ; Sloclap tie level organisation to combat: "similar dynamics from the organization of our levels to the use of weapons in combat". [summary] https://www.nintendolife.com/features/we-had-a-lot-to-learn-sloclap-talks-bringing-sifu-to-switch
- **Doom (2016/Eternal), the room serves the tool:** push-forward combat "stares down genre conventions like regenerating health, reload and cover, which puts the onus on the player to gather resources from enemies" [read] https://gdcvault.com/play/1024940/Embracing-Push-Forward-Combat-in ; Stratton: "You constantly have to move ... If you stand still, you will die"; glory kills make enemies drop more ammo and health; arenas as "skate parks" [read] https://bethesda.net/en-NZ/news/inside-doom's-brutally-fast-single-player-campaign

## 2. The ride that pays

- **Lucio (Overwatch): the wall is simply faster.** Wall Ride: +30 % movement speed while riding (40 % before the 2019-10-15 patch), plus a speed burst on release. [summary] https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/L%C3%BAcio
- **Titanfall 2: speed is kept and grown on the wall.** After the tech test Respawn raised max and base wall-run speed, air speed and the acceleration jumping off walls, fixed momentum loss while wall-running, and said the changes served gunplay (players were being shot from behind). [summary] https://careers.ea.com/games/titanfall/titanfall-2/news/tech-test-and-feedback ; the Jump Kit gives "a significant boost in speed while running along walls" [summary] https://primagames.com/eguides/titanfall-2-eguide/multiplayer/pilot-fundamentals ; wall-run 1.75 s base (3.5 s with the Enhanced Parkour Kit) [summary] https://titanfall.fandom.com/wiki/Enhanced_Parkour_Kit
- Titanfall 2 MP also made maps tighter ("tightening maps, designing abilities that encourage teamwork") and the gravity change makes airborne pilots EASIER to target: air is not automatically safe. [read] https://gameinformer.com/games/titanfall_2/b/playstation4/archive/2016/06/20/whats-the-same-whats-different-in-titanfall-2-multiplayer
- **ULTRAKILL pays for being off the floor in a kill-scored fight:** the movement MULTIPLIER rises 2.0/s up to 3x while sliding or airborne and falls back to 1x in 0.2 s on the ground. [read] https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Style
- **Ghostrunner:** arenas carry wall-run surfaces to dodge and reposition [summary] https://Techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/ghostrunner-review ; designers watched players to predict routes and tuned encounters so no single approach is too obvious; enemies' strengths and weaknesses make navigation a puzzle. [summary] https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/ghostrunner-is-one-of-the-best-looking-coolest-cyberpunk-games-on-the-market
- **Neon White:** Smackdown took 50+ iterations; it was the first level built for replays, alternate paths and shortcuts. [summary] https://gameinformer.com/feature/2022/11/25/how-a-neon-white-level-is-made

## 3. Keeping the gun from answering everything

- **Score decays on repetition (ULTRAKILL freshness):** each weapon variant 0-10; FRESH (7.5-10) 1.5x, USED 1.0x, STALE (1-5) 0.5x, DULL (0-1) 0.0x; each attack or bonus costs that variant 0.7 and gives every other weapon 0.5; unheld weapons regain 0.1/s; bosses decay it 1.5x. [read] https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Style
- **Health only from up close (ULTRAKILL):** blood healing encourages aggression [summary] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultrakill ; no medkits [summary] https://techraptor.net/gaming/previews/ultrakill-preview ; 35 % of damage becomes Hard Damage (lost max health) on Standard+. [summary] https://ultrakill.fandom.com/wiki/Hard_Damage
- **Fewer free resources, the tool refills them (Doom Eternal):** fewer health packs and ammo dumps, distribution "tuned and tightened" [read] https://www.engadget.com/2019-06-14-doom-eternal-hands-on-e3-2019.html ; resources come from enemies (the GDC overview above).
- **A part the right tool breaks (Doom Eternal):** the Arachnotron's turret is its primary attack; breaking it removes that attack; not required but it "makes life easier", and not every demon has one. [summary] https://www.gamestar.de/artikel/doom-eternal-daemonenzerstoerung-soll-kaempfe-taktischer-machen,3333370.html ; the Marauder's stun window fits one shot of most weapons, two if you swap: a timed window that a specific play answers. [summary] https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/doom-eternals-director-says-the-marauder-is-good-actually/
- **Armour / shields the tool breaks:** Bulletstorm's kick breaks armour (postmortem above, [read]); Ghostrunner adds shield generators to destroy (Unreal interview above, [summary]).

## 4. Ten room rules (numbers are OUR proposed thresholds; the sources give the principle, not the number)

R1. **Clear throw corridor.** For each designed punch spot P and its pit, the corridor from P to the pit's far rim, 1.5 m wide, holds no spawn cell, queue cell or enemy path waypoint; in sim, the first thing a thrown body touches is the pit (or the planted target of R5) in >= 90 % of throws. Sources: Bulletstorm (hazard in the kick's line; movable hazards), Dark Messiah, ULTRAKILL + FALL.
R2. **Pit inside the real throw, catching the long one.** Near rim 0.5-2.0 m from P along the punch direction (inside the measured 0.3-2.5 m play throws), and the pit runs >= 14 m or ends at a backstop wall that drops an overshoot into it, so the 11-14 m clean throw also lands. Sources: ULTRAKILL bottomless-pit bonuses (FALL / OUT OF BOUNDS catch any length); Dark Messiah bottomless pits. The backstop is our derivation.
R3. **The approach lane passes the rim.** Every melee chaser's walk from spawn to the player's hold zone passes within 2 m of a pit rim at a point where player, enemy and pit are collinear within 30 deg (the player faces the pit to punch). Sources: Doom push-forward (enemies come to the player as resources), ULTRAKILL simple predictable AI (80.lv), Dark Messiah (enemies near drops), Ghostrunner (designers predict routes).
R4. **Queues off the throw line.** Queue and spawn cells sit >= 4 m lateral to every R1 corridor or behind the player; at most 2 bodies are released into a lane at a time. Source: derived from R1 (no source states it; SKYGRIND's own measurement that queues burst the throw).
R5. **The burst is a planted target, not an accident.** Where a lane ends in bodies instead of a pit, a 2-3 enemy cluster stands 4-8 m down the corridor with no pit in front of it, and a burst kill is scored at least as high as a pit kill. Sources: ULTRAKILL FRIENDLY FIRE 200/250 > FALL 80; Bulletstorm chain reactions.
R6. **A guard only the verb breaks.** The room's verb-target enemy carries a guard (shield / armour / stagger state) that LAUNCH removes; measured gun-only time-to-kill from engagement range >= 2x the punch-and-throw time. Sources: Bulletstorm kick breaks armour; Doom Eternal weak points and the Marauder window; Ghostrunner shield generators.
R7. **Chasers arrive faster than the gun kills them.** Chasers enter from out of sight (behind cover or a ledge) within 6-10 m of the hold zone and reach punch range (<= 3 m) in <= 2 s; with a gun-only bot, >= 50 % of chasers reach punch range alive. Sources: Doom "if you stand still, you will die" and no cover play; ULTRAKILL (threat despite mobility).
R8. **The verb pays a resource the gun does not.** A LAUNCH or ride kill returns ammo or a cooldown refund (health only if GG allows it: CORE bans passive in-round regen, GG said regen only at round end); a gun kill returns none of it; and the room's score uses a repetition multiplier like freshness (gun-only clear <= 0.5x a mixed clear). Sources: Doom chainsaw ammo / glory-kill drops / tightened pickups; ULTRAKILL blood healing and freshness numbers; Bulletstorm "reward people for being creative".
R9. **The ride is the faster route between forced fight zones.** Spawns alternate between two zones >= 15 m apart so the player must cross; the wall line between them is >= 25 % faster than the best floor route (Lucio's +30 %), with no floor shortcut under it. Sources: Lucio Wall Ride; Titanfall 2 wall-run speed and momentum changes; Neon White shortcuts.
R10. **The ride holds the kill angle.** At least one enemy group per ride room stands where it is hittable only from the wall line (behind a parapet, on a ledge, or out of floor sight), or the wall is above melee reach so the rider takes no melee hits; in sim, >= 40 % of that room's kills land from the ride. Sources: ULTRAKILL airborne MULTIPLIER (pays off-floor kills); Ghostrunner wall-runs to dodge / reposition; Titanfall 2 note that airborne is targetable (so safety must come from geometry, not from being in the air).

## 5. What is NOT supported by sources

- Every numeric threshold in section 4 (1.5 m, 0.5-2.0 m, 30 deg, 4 m, >= 2x, <= 2 s, 25 %, 40 %) is ours, not a source's. Only Lucio's 30 % and ULTRAKILL's freshness / multiplier / style numbers come from the wikis.
