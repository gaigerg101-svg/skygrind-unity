# Lighting scout (forge72): flicker, z-fighting, readability, banding
Web research 2026-10-27, Unity 6000.3 URP Forward+. Tags: [DOC] Unity manual, [TRK] Unity issue tracker, [FORUM] community thread, [BLOG] practitioner write-up, [REF] encyclopedia, standard or paper, [INF] my inference with no source (test it). "(snippet)" = I saw the claim in a search result but could not open the page.
Gaps first: I found no primary source for ULTRAKILL, DUSK or Hi-Fi Rush lighting rules, none for key-to-fill ratios, none for minimum-luminance numbers in a shipped game. Section 3 says what exists instead.

## Ranking by impact for this game (procedural boxes, custom Stone shader, Forward+, dozens of point lights)
1. Stone shader contract: Forward+ light loop, screen UV, DepthOnly pass, fog (1a). It would explain "rooms inconsistent" and flicker on some surfaces only.
2. Coplanar boxes: remove or merge coincident faces in the builder, near plane 0.1-0.3, Offset only on thin decals (2).
3. Ambient SH differs between renderers or rooms: light probe usage, runtime ambient changes (1e).
4. Additional-light shadow atlas overflow and the number of shadow-casting lights (1c).
5. Directional shadow shimmer and acne (1d).
6. Bloom on thin bright geometry; TAA / MSAA limits with a camera stack (1g).
7. Reflection probe popping on Lit boxes (1f).
8. Per-camera light cap, tile overflow, Forward+ engine bugs (1b).
9. Boss-room darkness: per-fight Post Exposure + ambient floor + rim and floor accents (3, 5).
10. Banding and crushed blacks: HDR, Grading Mode, LUT size, dithering, tonemapper (5).

## 1. Flicker and popping: cause, detection, fix
### 1a. Stone shader must follow the Forward+ and depth-pass contract (rank 1)
- Forward+ in 6000.3 needs `#pragma multi_compile _ _CLUSTER_LIGHT_LOOP`, pass tag LightMode=UniversalForward, InputData with positionWS, normalWS, viewDirectionWS and normalizedScreenSpaceUV = GetNormalizedScreenSpaceUV(positionCS), a separate `#if USE_CLUSTER_LIGHT_LOOP` loop for non-main directional lights, then LIGHT_LOOP_BEGIN(GetAdditionalLightsCount()). [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/Manual/urp/use-built-in-shader-methods-additional-lights-fplus.html
- Older URP used `_FORWARD_PLUS` / `USE_FORWARD_PLUS`; a shader on the wrong keyword runs the per-object loop and ignores most lights. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/forward-not-working-with-custom-shader/912819
- A wrong normalizedScreenSpaceUV (built from a clip position in the fragment stage) made spot and point lights show only at some camera angles and screen areas, on 6000.0.34f1, 6000.0.59f1 and 6000.2.8f1. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/urp-forward-additional-lights-only-rendering-in-specific-camera-angles/1690665
- Detect: put one Stone box and one URP Lit box under the same point light, orbit the camera, compare. Any difference is the shader. [INF]
- Unity: "make sure that every shader pass renders the same number of fragments in the same positions", share vertex code between passes by include, add DepthOnly and DepthNormals passes (missing ones break Depth Priming and SSAO). [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/materials-and-shaders/shaders/writing-custom-shaders-urp/writing-shaders-urp/depth-only
- Diagnostic: Universal Renderer > Depth Priming Mode = Disabled; priming does not work with MSAA or Deferred (snippet). [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/urp/urp-universal-renderer.html
- A depth-prepass vs forward mismatch caused flicker in a custom SRP; the tracker's workaround was less aggressive near/far or one shared shader (snippet). [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity3d.com/issues/flickering-caused-by-z-fighting-with-depth-prepass-with-sv-depth-in-custom-srp
- Fog on a custom shader needs `multi_compile_fog`, ComputeFogFactor(positionCS.z) in the vertex stage, MixFog(color.rgb, fogFactor) in the fragment stage; without them Stone and Lit fog differently. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/how-to-disable-fog-in-lit-material-shadergraph-urp/935842 and https://scrapbox.io/unishader/Fog_%E3%81%AB%E5%AF%BE%E5%BF%9C%E3%81%95%E3%81%9B%E3%82%8B

### 1b. Light caps, tiles, engine bugs
- Forward+: no per-object limit, per-camera limit 256 on desktop, Main Light always counts; raise or lower it with MAX_VISIBLE_LIGHT_COUNT_DESKTOP in an embedded URP Config package (it drives shader compile time). [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/render-pipelines/universal-render-pipeline/introduction/urp-concepts/rendering-paths/forward-plus-rendering-path-limitations
- Under Forward+ Unity ignores URP Asset > Lighting > Main Light, Additional Lights and Per Object Limit; the screen is split into tiles and each box is lit only by its tile's lights. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/render-pipelines/universal-render-pipeline/introduction/urp-concepts/rendering-paths/forward-rendering-paths
- Forward: 9 lights per object, additional lights per camera 256 desktop, 32 mobile, 16 ES 3.0. [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/Manual/urp/lighting/light-limits-in-urp.html
- If visible lights exceed the cap Unity disables some, and a disabled shadow-casting light makes its shadow flicker; count with `renderingData.cullResults.visibleLights.Length`. A few dozen lights is far under 256, so this is not the likely cause. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/lighting-overview/lighting/shadows-in-urp/shadows-troubleshooting-urp
- Engine bug: "overflow in the z bins on forward+ making light disappear or clipped", fixed in 2022.3.16f1 and 2023.3.0a15; none known for 6000.3. [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity.com/issues/12917/light-flickering-when-using-forward-on-mobile
- Tile overflow: a tiled renderer omits lights when a tile hits its max, "very obvious visually", worst when the light is in the adjacent tile (custom SRP, tile 64 px, max 31 there). Unity's URP per-tile cap and tile size: not found. [BLOG] https://catlikecoding.com/unity/custom-srp/3-1-0/
- Detect: Rendering Debugger > Material Override = Lighting Complexity shows lights per tile. [DOC] same forward-rendering-paths page. Meta's fork reads 32 lights per tile as the budget (colours green to white). [DOC-fork] https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/unity/unity-forward-plus-rendering/
- Fix: shrink Light.range to the room, avoid large overlapping ranges that bleed through walls, fewer real-time lights per room. [INF] An unsolved report: 150 spotlights flickered as the camera moved in Forward+ and Deferred; replies suggested near plane 0.1, fewer real-time lights, baked or Mixed. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/flickering-on-off-lights-when-movin-urp/920257
- Light Culling Mask failed in Forward+ before 2022.3.21f1; use URP Asset > Lighting > Use Rendering Layers for per-room light sets. [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity3d.com/issues/urp-light-culling-mask-does-not-work-when-using-forward-plus-rendering-path
- Reference: Doom 2016 uses 16x8 screen cells x 24 depth slices = 3072 clusters, up to 256 lights per cluster. [BLOG] https://www.adriancourreges.com/blog/2016/09/09/doom-2016-graphics-study/

### 1c. Additional-light shadow atlas (rank 4)
- All spot and point shadows share one atlas; a point light costs 6 maps; a 1024 atlas holds sixteen 256 maps. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/lighting-overview/lighting/shadows-in-urp/shadow-resolution-urp
- Overflow makes URP lower resolution and warn: "Reduced additional punctual light shadows resolution by 4 to make 6 shadow maps fit in the 2048x2048 shadow atlas" (snippet). Default resolution tiers 256 / 512 / 1024 (PR snippet: https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/pull/2126). Expect shadow resolution to pop as lights enter or leave range. [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity.com/issues/691/reduced-additional-punctual-light-shadows-resolution-warnings-are-being-thrown-on-playmode-enterexit
- Fix: raise URP Asset > Lighting > Additional Lights > Shadow Atlas Resolution, set Cast Shadows off on most point lights, keep shadows for the few nearest. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/analysis/graphics-performance-profiling/in-urp/optimize-for-better-performance
- Detect: grep the console for the warning above; Frame Debugger shows the atlas. Doom 2016 caches shadow maps and redraws only changed ones. [BLOG] adriancourreges URL above.

### 1d. Directional shadow shimmer and acne (rank 5)
- Shadows flicker far from the origin or camera: Project Settings > Graphics > Culling Settings > Camera-Relative Culling (tick Shadows). [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/2022.3/Documentation/Manual/shadow-distance.html
- Acne, peter-panning, light leaking = bias: URP Asset > Shadows, or per light Shadows > Bias = Custom, Depth and Normal; high bias detaches shadows. [DOC] shadows-troubleshooting-urp URL in 1b.
- Shimmer on camera move: a thread ties it to the first cascade size; URP has no cascade blending (snippet). [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/urp-shadow-cascades-weird-bug-behaviour-in-build/1583700 and [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/cn/2022.3/Manual/render-pipelines-feature-comparison.html
- Conservative Enclosing Sphere widens the cascade culling sphere (more aliasing); Unity's perf table says enable it. Try both, measure with the flicker metric in 4. [DOC] https://docs-alpha.unity3d.com/ScriptReference/Rendering.ScriptableCullingParameters-conservativeEnclosingSphere.html
- Texel snapping for URP is not documented; a gamedev.net poster fixed rotation shimmer with it in own code. [FORUM] https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/707159-stable-cascaded-shadow-maps

### 1e. Ambient SH, light probes (rank 3)
- Renderer Light Probes = Off: "The Scene's ambient probe is provided to the shader"; BlendProbes interpolates a probe. Boxes with mixed settings near a probe group get different ambient. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/script-reference/unityengine/rendering/lightprobeusage
- A tracker report: renderers set to Off still sampled Adaptive Probe Volumes. If APV is in any scene, check it. [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity.com/issues/10335/meshrenderers-using-off-for-the-lightprobes-option-still-sample-apv
- Fix: one probe usage for every level renderer (all Off), no stray probe groups; lerp trilight colours per frame when changing rooms instead of snapping. [INF]
- DynamicGI.UpdateEnvironment renders the sky and projects to SH on the GPU and is slow; cache RenderSettings.ambientProbe. [TRK] https://issuetracker.unity.com/issues/8117/dynamicgiupdateenvironment-should-update-default-reflection and [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/can-i-cache-results-of-dynamicgi-updateenvironment/148650

### 1f. Reflection probes (rank 7)
- At most two probes per object; order by Importance, then smaller volume, then covered area; Blend Distance above half the extent never reaches 100%; skybox fills any shortfall; Forward+ evaluates per pixel. A third probe entering the top two jumps. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/lighting-overview/lighting/reflection-probes/introduction
- Shader Graph Reflection Probe node flickers in Forward+ (samples an unset texture; blows out with bloom). [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/forward-broken-with-reflection-probe-node-in-shadergraph/915776
- Fix: set Importance explicitly, no equal-Importance overlaps, or turn Environment Reflections off on Lit boxes. [INF]

### 1g. Bloom, emissive, AA (rank 6)
- Bloom Threshold is gamma-space (default 0.9), Clamp default 65472, High Quality Filtering "reduces flicker" (bicubic). [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/post-processing-and-full-screen-effects/urp/effect-list/bloom
- A tracker report closed as by design: the mesh was one pixel wide and lost intensity after the bloom downscale blend (snippet). [TRK] https://issuetracker-mig.prd.it.unity3d.com/issues/2d-urp-global-volume-bloom-post-processing-flickers-when-object-moves-in-the-view-space
- Fix: bright strips at least 3 px wide at 1080p or faded by distance, High Quality Filtering on, lower Clamp for emissive spikes. [INF]
- TAA cannot be used with MSAA, Camera Stacking or Dynamic Resolution, and ghosts; MSAA does not fix specular or texture aliasing. This game has a camera stack, so TAA is out. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/render-pipelines/universal-render-pipeline/anti-aliasing
- TAA edge flicker mitigations (less sharpening, more Anti-Flicker, higher Base Blend Factor, lower Jitter Scale); thin 1 px edges suit MSAA or SMAA (snippet). [TRK] https://issuetracker-mig.prd.it.unity3d.com/issues/flickering-along-the-gameobject-edge-is-noticeable-when-post-anti-aliasing-is-set-to-taa
- CRT scanline moire and emissive-material flicker: no URP source found; test with the still-camera metric in 4.

## 2. Z-fighting on coplanar procedural boxes
- Causes: coplanar polygons, round-off, large far/near ratio, near plane close to the eye. Higher depth precision alone does not fix coplanar faces. [REF] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-fighting
- Fix 1, geometry (the only complete one): never emit coincident faces; drop hidden faces; merge adjacent coplanar boxes into one quad or mesh. Wikipedia lists "moving polygons apart" first. Same URL.
- Fix 2, offset growth: grow the overlay or inner box by more than the depth quantum at the farthest visible distance of its room, then confirm with the frame test in 4. [INF]
- Fix 3, near plane: a poster moved near from 0.1 to about 0.6 and the fighting vanished, raising far from 1000 to 100000 changed nothing; others advise a 24-bit depth buffer and split near/far cameras (a camera stack already exists). [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/how-does-the-clipping-plane-relate-to-z-fighting/607929
- Fix 4, shader Offset factor, units: both -1 to 1, negative pulls toward the camera, "the size of that minimum value varies by device". Use only on thin decal or trim layers. [DOC] https://docs.unity3d.com/6/Documentation/Manual/SL-Offset.html
- Reversed Z: DX, Metal, Vulkan store depth 1.0 near to 0.0 far (UNITY_REVERSED_Z); in reverse-Z a near-clipped triangle gets the maximum offset and the offset is constant across a polygon, so big polygons and shared edges are inconsistent. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/current/manual/materials-and-shaders/shaders/writing-custom/shader-writing/sl/writing-shader-writing-shader-programs-hlsl/sl-platform-differences and [FORUM] https://irrlicht.sourceforge.io/forum/viewtopic.php?p=301707
- Find every coplanar pair offline: bucket faces by quantised plane (normal, d), test coplanarity with vertex-to-plane distance, then 2D overlap per pair; plain triangle intersection missed these cases. [FORUM] https://community.khronos.org/t/detect-overlaping-polygons/39678
- For boxes [INF]: for each pair with intersecting or touching AABBs, for each axis compare face planes, flag |d1 - d2| below the room's depth quantum (not just exactly equal) with positive overlap area; same-side faces only. Fail the build on any pair.

## 3. Readable dark games: what is actually documented
- ULTRAKILL: the one first-party line is "The graphics are PS1 style" (developer reply). A fan thread quotes the developer on keeping flashes scarce so a flash keeps its value. No source for its vertex lighting or fog per layer. [FORUM] https://steamcommunity.com/app/1229490/discussions/0/3002177675804624601 and https://steamcommunity.com/app/1229490/discussions/2/3734079155590153286
- DUSK: built in Unity to look like Quake; no lighting write-up found. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dusk_(video_game)
- Doom 2016: the study has clusters, a shadow cache, 128x128 probes ("several dozens" per level) and half-res SSAO, but nothing on readability. Doom Eternal color-codes pickups (green armor, blue glory-kill health) so they read at speed (snippet). https://www.adriancourreges.com/blog/2016/09/09/doom-2016-graphics-study/ and https://shacknews.com/article/116635/hell-razer-the-making-of-doom-eternal?page=5
- Hi-Fi Rush: only the GDC 2024 abstract (toon lights, static shadow maps, light-probe GI). https://schedule.gdconf.com/session/3d-toon-rendering-in-hi-fi-rush/899802
- Valve (best evidence): readability by color, then silhouette, then detail; warm vs cool teams; shadows cool and "never going black"; fog was lightened because dark fog hid silhouettes; L4D desaturated colors below a threshold so pickups pop; grain only in dark areas; players "will go wherever there's lights". [BLOG] https://gamedeveloper.com/art/in-depth-how-valve-makes-art-to-enhance-gameplay ; rim highlights plus luminance and hue shifts, highest contrast at chest level (snippet): https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Illustrative_Rendering_in_Team_Fortress_2 and https://cdn.fastly.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2008/MIGS08_ConnectingVisualsToGameplay.pdf
- GDC 2018 "How to Light a Level" (Robert Yang): ambient so the world "isn't pitch black"; key, fill, rim; setpieces and enemies via rim and background lights; light the same type of thing the same way; darker albedo makes rooms much darker, aim at upper-midtone albedo checked with a histogram; fill lights set to "Not Important" so they skip specular. No numeric ratios given. [BLOG] https://www.blog.radiator.debacle.us/2018/03/gdc-2018-how-to-light-level-slides-and.html
- Level Design Book: albedo roughly 50-100% brightness, ambient is the default minimum, critical path lit most, lights tied to fixtures, similar spaces lit alike. [BLOG] https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/lighting
- Numbers that exist: walls 35-55% gray and actors in a different value band, value before hue, grayscale squint test; for boss rooms fix readability with rim or floor contrast only in warning zones, not whole-room exposure (SEO blog, low confidence). [BLOG] https://gamineai.com/blog/top-down-dungeon-lighting-2026-12-contrast-rules-readable-combat-spaces and https://gamineai.com/blog/cast-shadow-noise-vs-telegraph-clarity-top-down-boss-rooms-lighting-sanity-pass-2026
- Proposed rules for SKYGRIND [INF, synthesis; no source gives these numbers]:
  - R1 One light recipe per room kind (same ambient trilight value, same key intensity band); zones differ by hue only. Measure room medians (4).
  - R2 Ambient is the floor: no combat-room floor, wall or ledge reads below a luminance floor taken from the best current room.
  - R3 Enemies separate from stone by value first, then hue; keep cyan, red-orange and magenta out of ambient and fill, tint fill violet-blue.
  - R4 Boss arena: key and rim from behind or above the boss, floor value lifted around telegraphs and platforms, fog not darker than the floor, a per-fight Volume for exposure.

## 4. Measurable checks on screenshots
- Luminance: Y = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B on linearised channels (sRGB threshold 0.04045 per the W3C errata). Run on the final tonemapped frame, and again with the CRT pass off. [REF] https://www.w3.org/WAI/GL/wiki/Relative_luminance
- Histogram and dark share [INF]: report p1, p5, p50, p95 of Y; share of pixels with Y < 0.02; share > 0.95 (clipping). Judge each room against the reference room, not an absolute number; consistency = spread of room medians and p5.
- Local contrast: RMS contrast = standard deviation of normalised intensity; Weber (I - Ib) / Ib for an enemy against its background using an ID mask; Michelson only for periodic patterns. [REF] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contrast_(vision)
- Temporal flicker, still camera [INF protocol]: freeze the sim, fixed seed, TAA off, capture 60 frames; per-pixel luminance difference to the previous frame, count pixels whose difference flips sign repeatedly, report mean and p99.9 of |dY| and the flipped-pixel count. Prior art: frame-difference flash counting. [REF] https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.09491 and patent https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/6154258 ; perceptual version: https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~rkm38/pdfs/denes2020flicker_model.pdf
- Pass rule from a practitioner: cap the number of pixels moving more than one value step per frame; review at 0.75 speed. [BLOG] gamineai top-down URL in 3.
- Z-fight from frames [INF, rests on near/far dependence in Wikipedia]: render each room pose twice, near 0.05 vs 0.3, plus a half-pixel camera jitter; flag pixels that differ in a high-frequency stripe pattern while the rest matches; map to a box pair through an ID or depth pass; cross-check against the offline pair finder in 2.
- ML glitch detectors exist (EA: 86.8% accuracy on texture glitches; AMD: 84% seen games, 69% unseen) but none cover z-fighting, so use the rule-based checks. https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/view/7409 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2011.15103

## 5. Banding, crushed blacks, lifted but not grey shadows
- Banding in dark gradients: forum fixes are URP Asset HDR on, HDR Precision 64 bits, Post-processing Grading Mode = High Dynamic Range, LUT size 32 or 64, camera Dithering; test with post-processing off first. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/why-light-fall-off-looks-so-bad-in-urp-on-pc/1710175
- The URP editor tooltip: the minimal recommended LUT size for HDR grading is 32, lower gives banding and posterization (snippet); the same 16 to 32 fix in another thread. [FORUM] https://discussions.unity.com/t/lightmapper-being-really-weird-and-baking-purple-patches-in-completely-dark-rooms-color-banding/287853
- Unity's performance table recommends Grading Mode = Low Dynamic Range and the lowest LUT size: the opposite of what banding needs. Check the asset was not set that way. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/analysis/graphics-performance-profiling/in-urp/optimize-for-better-performance
- Tonemapping: Neutral has minimal hue and saturation impact; ACES is more contrasted and changes hue and saturation. For fixed cyan / red-orange / magenta roles use Neutral. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/post-processing-and-full-screen-effects/urp/effect-list/tonemapping
- Lift without grey: Color Adjustments > Post Exposure is in EV and applied after HDR effects, before tonemapping, so a per-room Volume lifts a boss arena without touching lights. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/post-processing-and-full-screen-effects/urp/effect-list/color-adjustments
- Lift / Gamma / Gain follows ASC CDL and Lift acts on dark tones; Shadows Midtones Highlights adds Shadow Limits Start and End. Tint the shadow wheel toward dusk violet-blue (Valve: cool shadows, never black) instead of raising neutral gain; equal lift on all channels is what reads grey. [DOC] https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/post-processing-and-full-screen-effects/urp/effect-list/lift-gamma-gain and https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.3/manual/post-processing-and-full-screen-effects/urp/effect-list/shadows-midtones-highlights
- Not checked: where tonemapping sits relative to the CRT pass, and whether Fast sRGB/Linear conversion matters here (perf table says enable, one forum says try disabling).
