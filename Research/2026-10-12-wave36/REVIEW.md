# Wave 36 review: an honest critique of n35-r4 (2026-10-12)

Sources: GG's 14 screenshots (15:49-16:00), GG-REQUEST.md, two code audits (file:line below), research/design-web.md (web,
URLs there), research/rea-ultrakill-combat.md (the ULTRAKILL decompile, numbers only). Everything here was read or seen; what was
not checked says so.

## The verdict in one paragraph
GG is right, and the cause is mostly the method, not one bug. 35 nights have added verbs, rows and rooms on top of each other and
judged them with harness lines (PASS / GREEN / 10/10 walks), while GG judges fun. A row can be GREEN and still feel wrong: the
charge row made every pistol tap fire late, and every check passed. The levels are the clearest case: each n35 level is a 1.4-2.2 MB
file generated by `gen-n35 = gen-n34 + … = gen-n33 + … = gen-n30`, so props from six nights pile up with nobody ever designing the
room as a whole. The props have reasons in the generator comments ("two low covers", "a pillar to circle", "a tease"), but the
player can't see any of those reasons. The bosses have mechanics on paper (only a wall-ride hit hurts THE LANTERN; THE STAG's back
is open after a slam) but the screen never says so: you are cut into a separate arena 2.4 km away, a block shape does something,
and a line of text asks you to understand it.

## Root causes (what the code says)
1. **The pistol fires late.** charge35 (on by default): a press only starts a hold; a tap fires on RELEASE (Core/Guns/Charge35.cs:114-142,
   175-185). Only a click shorter than one 8 ms sim step fires on the press; real clicks are 50-100 ms. Every KESTREL shot comes
   50-100 ms after the finger, and the crosshair and kick come with it. This is "the pistol is not satisfying anymore", a
   regression from NIGHT35's own fix.
2. **Shots don't leave the gun.**
   - Nails, rockets and grenades start at the eye: the sim origin, drawn as is.
   - Hitscan tracers start at the muzzle, but before that shot's kick moves the muzzle (SurvivalGame.Render.cs:40 vs :103). The muzzle flash is drawn after the kick, so the line and the flash come apart.
   - The THUNDERCLAP slug draws 7 fake pellet lines around one real line (WeaponFx.cs:254-272).
3. **Hits aren't crunchy.**
   - A KESTREL body hit gets no freeze at all; freezes exist only for kills, headshots and heavy hits (ImpactRules.cs:19-30).
   - Enemies have a 0.03 s white flash and a body spring, but no stagger pose and no knockback you can see.
   - The gauntlet is a 10 cm block with a jab (BlockyKit.cs:568-581).
   - ULTRAKILL: every punch hit freezes 0.1 s; the revolver beam 0.05 s.
4. **The wall ride climbs.**
   - On attach the ride keeps any upward speed (Mover.cs:1023, `if (velocity.y < 0) velocity.y = 0`).
   - A tap ride runs at 0.15 gravity (wallRide.gravityMult) for 1.2 s, so a jump into a wall climbs about 8 m (estimate, not measured). That is "you hook on and slide up".
   - Titanfall-style rides grip near level and then sink, keeping the speed you arrived with.
5. **The boss is a cut, not a place.**
   - Every boss except THE LOOM begins with `world.Respawn(bossSpawn)` into an arena at x 2400-3200 (Campaign24.cs:571-575), a hard cut (feel.Cut).
   - There is no reveal, no walk-in, no arena you saw coming.
6. **Bosses explain themselves in text.**
   - Their rules are real but invisible: full damage only while riding (THE LANTERN), the back only after a slam (THE STAG), the joints only within 6 m (THE LOOM).
   - There is no tell colour, no pose per attack, and no punish window that the screen shows.
7. **No ease-in.**
   - L2 and L3 open in a fight (iceline i1a: a tank at 0.6 s; kiln k1: 8 husks then 4 swarmers); L4 opens with 7 enemies and 2 lava pits.
   - Only L1, L5 and L6 have a calm first room.
8. **The rooms are stamps and leftovers.**
   - The same 18x36 RIDE HALL (two 10.5 m panels over two lava pools, a pillar, two crates) is stamped into every n35 level (n35a-lib.cjs:45-88).
   - The other rooms are n30 rooms with n32, n33, n34 and n35 passes added on top.
   - Lava was kept away from enemies' paths "because of bot lava deaths": lava as a decoration, not a threat to anyone but the player.
9. **The parry is a death tip.**
   - GG wants "an understanding of parry mechanics"; the game has a parry (the gauntlet's ZL parry, glob punch-back), but nothing teaches it, nothing marks what is parryable, and it pays nothing a player can see.
   - ULTRAKILL marks parryable attacks with a flash, and a parry heals, refills stamina and freezes time 0.25 s.
10. **Text placement.** Every teaching line and lesson shares the top slot with the room name, at 24 px, over the darkest part of the frame.

## What works (keep)
- L1's calm start, one verb per room (GG: "the only one that sort of shows").
- The grapple pull and the wall ride's speed ("fine, could be a little better").
- The look: the PS2 treatment, the dusk palette, the HUD shape (no complaint this wave).
- The verbs list (docs/VERBS.md) and the nothing-lost gate: the overhaul keeps every verb.

## The process critique (why this keeps happening)
- **Judged by harness, not by eye.** GREEN lines prove a rule fires, not that it feels good; GG has said so since w17 (L-001), and the
  nights still close on harness tables.
- **Breadth every night.** Each night touches all six levels and five bosses, so none gets finished. GG asked for six polished levels
  (w34); we keep shipping six unpolished ones.
- **Patch-on-patch levels.** GG said "complete arena overhaul from scratch, don't preserve anything" (w28). The files still descend
  from n30.
- **Builders can't see rooms.** Room geometry is typed as coordinates in .cjs files by agents that never look at the room from the
  player's eye before it ships.
