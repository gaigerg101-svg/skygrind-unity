## Clip-worthy features

What makes a moment travel, with the evidence found:

1. **Capture is already on the Deck.** Steam Game Recording records in the background, lets players trim and share an MP4,
   and has worked on Steam Deck since Nov 2024 (S41, S42). A game only has to make the moment happen and mark it. Steam's
   Timeline API, where a game adds markers to that recording (e.g. "P-RANK", "40-kill streak"), was not fetched this
   session: UNVERIFIED.
2. **A visible number that peaks.** Style ranks (ULTRAKILL, S12), a kill timer near zero (Mullet MadJack, S25), a speed
   number (Titanfall 2 chains, S40) put the drama on screen, so a 10-second clip explains itself. Presskit.gg's TikTok
   guide says satisfying mechanic loops and creator reactions beat polished trailers (S43).
3. **Ghosts and gated leaderboards.** Neon White shows a ghost and the world times only after the player clears / Aces
   the level, so the race starts when the player is ready (S22). Ghost-vs-me clips need nothing from the streamer.
4. **Shared seeds and per-difficulty boards.** Cyber Grind counts only official-rotation runs on its Steam boards (S11);
   a daily / weekly seed (already in SKYGRIND) makes "beat my score" fair.
5. **Community hunts.** BO7 Relics stayed unfound for weeks, which Treyarch counts as a success (S7); BO6 Directed mode
   doubled quest completions (S1). Secrets make videos, guides make retention.
6. **Dated content drops.** ULTRAKILL's Fraud drop took the peak from 28 k to 72 k, with the publisher telling players to
   "call out sick" (S9).
7. **Kill cams, photo mode, instant replay:** no 2024-2026 data was found in this session for solo games. UNVERIFIED.
   Note: a deterministic fixed-step sim seeded per run can replay a round from its inputs, so a "last 10 s" replay or a
   ghost costs a tape, not a video recorder (a design inference, not a measured claim).
