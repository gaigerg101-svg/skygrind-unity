## 2. Encounter size, mix, layers

Counts per fight (from the pages above and R1): a wave is 1 to 14 units; a room has 2 to 5 waves; totals are 3 (tutorial) up to 25 (2-1 Second Tower), 30 in 1-3's skull arena (R1).
- 0-x: Filth and Strays only, then one Schism or Malicious Face as the guest. 0-2 totals 31 Filth, 22 Strays (R1).
- 1-2: adds Streetcleaner as the new type, introduced in 2s and 3s, then recombined in the Final Arena. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/1-2
- 2-1: heavy types appear inside waves (Cerberus 1, Malicious Face 1) and the room totals reach 13 to 25. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-1
- 2-2: Soldiers (the wiki calls them "upgraded Strays") replace Strays; waves of 2, 3, 4 Soldiers teach parry. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-2

Layer order, as read: 0-1 one new thing per room; 1-1/1-2 types doubled (2 then 4, 2 then 3); 2-1 the room itself is the verb (climb, dash jump) and waves ride on it; 2-2 weapon/arm pickup rooms with a 5-Stray demonstration wave (Railcannon pickup). https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-2

## 3. Walls versus floor

No source states a "wall and floor trade" rule (not found). What the pages show:
- Floor is the danger: pits, fans, lava, crushers, grinders, acid, sand, in rooms 1-9 above. 2-1 towers have a pit or fan at the bottom, so falling is the cost of a mistake; bounce pads sit under the First Bridge Platform so a fall there is safe. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-1
- Walls are the route: 2-1 First Tower is climbed by wall-jump along metal framework; 1-2 Lava Room needs a wall-jump out through a ceiling hole. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/1-2
- Rule of thumb read from this: floor hazards tax standing still, wall surfaces buy height. Inference, not a quote.

## 4. Empty rest rooms and hazard floors

- 2-1 Maintenance Tunnel, First Bridge Platform, Final Bridges: 0 enemies; they teach the Knuckleblaster, fall recovery (bounce pads), then close the level. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-1
- 0-5: intro stair with two inactive statues, then Lava Foundry ("first level to contain lava"), a three-crusher passage, a grinder pit, then the boss. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/0-5
- 1-4 before V2: no enemies at all; a mansion of key puzzles; a pit does 50 damage and teleports you back. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/1-4
- 4-4: golden sand does 10 damage per contact; sand, caved-in room and broken bridge have no enemies. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/4-4
- 3-2: seven rooms of pure traversal (eye climb, flesh bridge, acid hallway, vein slide...) with no enemies before Gabriel. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/3-2
- 2-4: subway tunnel and tram station, 0 enemies, before the Minos Arm. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-4
Pattern: the quiet stretch before a boss is hazards or puzzles, never fodder; 3 to 7 rooms long.

## 6. How a room teaches one verb (new examples)

1. 2-2 Laser Walkway: enemy waves are all Soldiers (projectile users) so the parry is the only answer; the arm swap is introduced in the same room. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-2
2. 2-1 Third Bridge Platform: a dash-jump prompt sits in a room whose gaps need it. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/2-1
3. 0-2: crushers introduced in a narrow passage with 2 Filth, then a square arena, then a corridor with two crushers and a fan: alone, then with enemies, then stacked. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/0-2:_The_Meatgrinder
4. 4-4 Whiplash Tutorial Arena: small arena, 3 enemies then 1 Malicious Face, right before the boss. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/4-4
5. 1-2 Red Skull Pickup: the tell is taught by on-screen text, then tested by 3 enemies in the same room. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/1-2
