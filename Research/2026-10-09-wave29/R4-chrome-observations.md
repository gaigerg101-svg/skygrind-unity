## 0-4 A ONE-MACHINE ARMY (a short linear gauntlet, "only three main arenas")
- Palette: hot orange / amber on dark grated metal; corridors are narrow with grated floors; the visuals are all glow from within the walls (the "glow in a closed room" GG likes).
- Structure: intro hallway -> first arena (10 Filth + 5 Strays, then 1 Malicious Face after a type dies) -> stairs hallway (a checkpoint, the first COOLING CHAMBER that locks the player in for a few seconds: a rest/transition room) -> rotating tunnel (a platform splitting a tunnel of hazardous rotating sections, 10 damage on contact; 6 Filth + 1 Stray, then 4 Strays after reaching the centre platform; a secret behind glass) -> pipes hallway (second cooling chamber) -> final arena (a large chamber with a GLASS FLOOR over a fan-blade pit; 6 Filth + 4 Strays + 2 Schisms, then 14 Filth; the floor can be broken to drop enemies into the pit) -> exit elevator.
- Rank requirements: S 2:20 and 53 kills, A 3:00 / 50, B 4:00 / 40, C 5:00 / 30. So a "short" level is 2-5 minutes and 30-53 kills. Challenge: slide 17 s uninterrupted (the level is a lesson in the slide).
- Secrets are reached by a verb (jump into a nook; slide under gaps onto a ledge beside the pit): the secrets reward the level's own mechanic.

## 0-5 CERBERUS (the boss level; "an enclosed cramped space")
- Order: intro staircase (two inactive statues: the boss is foreshadowed) -> LAVA FOUNDRY (a chasm with a lava river and a bridge) -> HAZARD PASSAGE (a low passage: you must SLIDE; three crushers in the middle need TIMING; a grinder pit around the corner) -> SMALLWAY (a short hallway with flowing-lava walls and a checkpoint terminal right before the door) -> the boss arena.
- So the approach to a boss is a verb-test (slide under timed crushers) and a checkpoint, then a cramped arena. Rank requirements: S 2:00, 2 kills (the two bosses), C 10:00.
- Lesson for SKYGRIND: the level before a boss should put the boss's verb on the player's body (the Glacier's level = skate lanes; the Lantern's = the wall-ride room), end with a lit short hallway and a checkpoint, then the boss room. Foreshadow the boss (statues, a glow, a sound-free light cue).

## 1-4 CLAIR DE LUNE (V2's level)
- "A test of the player's skill": a long stone pathway with a pit; FALLING INTO THE PIT DEALS 50 DAMAGE AND TELEPORTS YOU BACK TO SOLID GROUND (a hard-hit but safe fail: no death, no lost progress). SKYGRIND's hazard floor and tutorial wall-ride room should do the same (a damaging fall, a respawn on the spot, no level loss).
- Rank requirements: S 2:15 / 0 kills (the level is the boss, so time and style rank it).

## What this adds to NIGHT29 (the lead should fold these into `DESIGN29.md`)
1. Level length: 2-5 minutes and 30-55 kills for a level (0-4); a boss level can be 2-10 minutes (0-5), most of it the boss.
2. A COOLING-CHAMBER-style transition room between fights (a short sealed room that locks you in a few seconds, lit differently): a checkpoint and a breath.
3. A hazard that is also a TIMING lesson (crushers you slide through) before a boss.
4. A safe fail that costs health and returns you (the 50-damage pit): use it for the wall-ride room and the ice crossings.
5. Secrets and optional rewards that are reached with the level's own verb.
6. A floor that changes mid-fight (the glass floor over a pit): the Lantern's floor-is-lava cycle and the Glacier's frost trails are the same kind of idea.
