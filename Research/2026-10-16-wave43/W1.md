## G19 fusion43, the sim (measured)

- Rule (Core/Fusion43.cs; four one-line hooks in Combat.cs: the swing's and the punch's extra reach, `x Fusion43Pre(enemy)` inside each
  Damage call (exactly x1 off), `Fusion43Post` after it): a gauntlet swing hit or a punch hit is a FUSION when the player's horizontal speed
  is >= 14 m/s, or a dash runs or ended <= 0.25 s ago, or a wall jump (off a ride or a wall) was <= 0.35 s ago; at most one each 0.5 s.
  Then: damage x2 (x1.5 on a boss body), a 14 m/s knock for 0.3 s, Enemy.Stun 0.6 s (boss bodies ignore both), a hitstop request of
  0.22 s (0.28 s on a boss) through the sim's Hitstop with the cause "fusion43", and ONE `fusion43` event (N43Events.Fusion) with the reused
  Fusion43Event. While ready, a swing / punch with nothing in reach reaches +1 m. Numbers: design.n43.fusion43 (Tools/night/n43w1-config.cjs).
- ImpactRules (additive): a new tier Fusion43 (TierCount 14 -> 15, TierNames "fusion43", rank as Gauntlet42). The sim's "fusion43"
  request becomes it at the sim's length (it also covers a heavy41 request of the same step), out of the shared gap and minute like
  Gauntlet42, inside design.n43.fusion43.perMinute (0.08 = 4.8 s a minute) counted together with the gauntlet's freezes; it wins a tie.
  `n43weap fusion`: a husk hit 160 -> 220 ms (Fusion43), a husk kill 220 -> 220 ms (Fusion43 wins the tie), THE ICEBREAKER 240 -> 280 ms;
