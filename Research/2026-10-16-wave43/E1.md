## Door rules (rooms43)

- A fight room's door counts only the bodies the room spawned (waves, top-ups; CampaignWorld.Spawn). Stand-ins never hold the door; they hold the
  room's NEXT WAVE for at most `standInHoldS` (20 s) after the entry (NIGHT42's KESTREL-room order kept: the stand-ins, then the husks).
- A room's body out of the room's box (rect + nodes' box + 2 m) or under its floor (the lowest of spawn / nodes - 1.5 m) for 2 s ends: alive false,
  no kill event, no points.
- Top-ups at most 2. NIGHT42 bug found (kept as is with rooms43 off): with a verb lesson before an enemy lesson, the top-up cap's
  SkipPendingEnemyLessons skipped nothing and `lessonTopUpCap` fired every step (13 724 times in one harness room).
- A fight room whose own enemies are dead marks its lessons done after `lessonCapS` (8 s; event `lessonCap43`), then the door opens.
- `Campaign24.RoomLeft43()` = `SurvivalSim.N43RoomLeft`: own alive + inside a spawn tell + the waves still to come; -1 outside a locked fight (links,
  bosses, cleared rooms, rooms43 off). **For U1's counter43.**
- `roomClear43` (N43Events.RoomClear, payload the room id) as a fight room's door opens.
- `heavy` spawns as the TANK (heavyKind "tank": design.n29.tank, 600 hp, x10 to the gauntlet, x0.15 to the pistol). **K1 / the lead: level 3's kb3
  (x2) and level 5's v2 / v6 become tanks with rooms43 on** (a gauntlet check).
