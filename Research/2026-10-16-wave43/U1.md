# U1 (UI), NIGHT43: the HUD's visual language, then hud43 / counter43 / teach43 / bossbar43 / screens43

Builder U1. GG (G6, 2026-10-16): the UI looks "too generic and cheap, almost like basic placeholder or default AI-generated UI"; "Establish a
strong visual language for the game with better typography, hierarchy, spacing, colors, animation, effects, and thematic elements." Sources:
research/ui-art-web.md (A1-A4 the centre card, B5-B9 art direction, the blur, the health bar), research/bosses-web.md (A1-A3 the boss bar),
docs/PRINCIPLES.md 7 ("the UI moves a little like an old TV"), UnityTuning.Theme.cs (the faces and hues already in the game). Every number in
this section is a token: UI numbers live in `Assets/Scripts/Game/UnityTuning.Ui43.cs` (render / layout only, none is gameplay).

## 1. The visual language: "the dusk instrument"

SKYGRIND is speed at dusk: a navy-violet sky, an orange horizon, cyan things you can use, red-orange things that hurt you, on a PS2-era
screen. The UI is the player's instrument panel at that hour: thin, hard, slanted, lit from inside like a CRT, never a web page laid over the
game. One idea carries it: **the blade**, a parallelogram leaning at the display face's own italic (12 deg) with one 45 deg corner cut. Every
new element is a blade or is drawn from blades: the health cells, the boss bar, the counter's frame, the teach card, the shop cards, the
results panels. Nothing is a rounded rectangle; nothing is a plain rectangle.

### 1.1 Type: three faces, five roles (the faces already shipped: UnityTuning.Theme.cs)

| Role | Face | Sizes (px at 1080p) | Case / tracking | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HERO | Archivo ExtraCondensed Black Italic (`UiTheme.Display`) | 56 (HUD health), 75 (shop / results title), 133 (the results' one hero number) | as written; tracking 0 | the one number that matters on a screen |
| TITLE | Archivo ExtraCondensed Black Italic | 32 (boss name, counter number), 40 (the teach card's line) | caps; tracking 2 | names, the card's verb line |
| LABEL | Archivo ExtraBold (`UiTheme.BodyBold`) | 16 (HUD minimum, CLAUDE.md rule 8), 18, 24 | caps; tracking 8 | tags: LEFT, LEG 1 / 3, LOCKED, OPEN, a weapon's name |
| BODY | Archivo SemiBold (`UiTheme.Body`) | 20, 24 | sentence case | the teach card's second line, the boss hint, a shop item's line |
| RANK | Tourney Black Italic (`UiTheme.Rank`) | 40 (HUD), 100 (results grade) | caps | the style rank and the results' grade only |

Rules: at most 4 sizes per screen; the HERO is 2-3x anything else on its screen (ui-art-web B6, Persona 5's hierarchy by role); never two
display-face lines next to each other at the same size; numbers never in the body face. HUD floor 16 px, menus 18 px.

| DUSK | `colors.skyZenith` #100A2C | the backdrop tint: the blurred game multiplied by DUSK at 0.55 behind every full screen |

| CYAN | `colors.usable` #19D3E6 (+ DEEP / PALE / HOT ladder) | what the player can use NOW: a heal, a ready charge, a boss's OPEN window, the selected shop card, the key glyph on a teach card |
| DANGER | `colors.enemy` #FF4A1C | enemies and harm: the boss's HP, a hit's frame pulse, low health, the counter's accent (they are enemies) |

| STEEL | #8E97B5 | a boss that cannot be hurt now (LOCKED): low saturation, so it never reads as cyan (bosses-web A3, ULTRAKILL's protected bar) |

Saturation budget (ui-art-web C10): the HUD is mostly BONE and MUTED; one saturated colour per element at a time, and only when it means
something. Gradients only inside meters (Persona 5: flat fills).

### 1.3 Shape: the blade, the bracket, the cut

- **The blade:** a parallelogram leaning 12 deg (`ThemeSlantDeg`, the italic's angle; NIGHT33's 20 deg slash stays on the elements that keep
  it). Built with `UiSkew.Apply(img, tan 12)`. Health cells, the boss bar, the counter's pips, shop cards' accent bars.
- **The bracket (HUD frames):** a HUD element is framed, never filled: two 2 px corner brackets (an L at the top-left and a mirrored L at the
  bottom-right, 10 px arms) in MUTED. On an event the bracket, not the fill, takes the colour (a hit: DANGER; a heal: CYAN).
- **The cut (full screens):** panels are INK with the 45 deg cut at the top-right and bottom-left (`UiTheme.Cut`, 10 px) and a 4 px accent bar
  on the left edge in the panel's role colour (CYAN for the player's things, GOLD for points, DANGER for threats).
- **Spacing:** an 8 px grid; HUD inset 72 px left / right, 54 px top / bottom (Hud33Layout); panel padding 24 px; the gap between a label and
  its value 6 px.

### 1.4 The PS2 / old-TV treatment (PRINCIPLES 7)

- **Scanlines:** every fill and panel carries a 3 px scanline overlay (one row in three darker by 18 %), point-sampled, so the UI sits in the
  same CRT as the world (Crt29 / look35).
- **Colour steps:** meters fill in cells (10 health cells, 20 boss cells' worth of after-image steps), not smooth gradients.
- **The chroma split:** on a hit the hero number shows two hard copies offset 3 px (CYAN left, DANGER right) for 0.18 s: a CRT's
  misconvergence, not a glow.
- **No soft glows, no drop-shadow blur:** the shadow is NIGHT33's hard 2 px INK twin.

### 1.5 Motion rules (unscaled frame time: the ride + aim slow at 0.30 never slows the UI)

| Motion | Rule | Numbers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OPEN (a card, a panel, a boss bar) | the CRT pop: X grows first, then Y (ULTRAKILL HudOpenEffect, ui-art-web A2) | 0.08 s + 0.08 s, from 0.05 |
| CLOSE | Y collapses, then X | 0.05 s + 0.05 s |
| ENTRANCE (a full screen's parts) | staggered along the slant: each part comes in 60 px down-left with ease-out-back (10 % overshoot) | 0.15 s each, 0.05 s apart |
| TICK (a number changes) | a punch 1 -> 1.25 -> 1 and a 1-frame HOT flash | 0.15 s, at most once per 0.1 s |
| HIT (on the player) | the bracket pulses DANGER and the number splits; no screen-wide tint | 0.25 s decay |
| AFTER-IMAGE | the HOT chunk waits, then drains (bosses-web A2: ULTRAKILL's numbers) | waits 0.15 s; drains at (gap x 5 + 0.5) / s |
| LOOP | only two things ever move on their own: low health (the bracket breathes) and an OPEN boss window (its timer) | 1.2 Hz breathe |

Everything else is still. No slide-ups on every element, no hover pulses, no idle bobbing.

### 1.6 For F1 and A1 (hand-off)

- F1's "FUSION" HUD word: the HERO face (Archivo ExtraCondensed Black Italic) at 56 px, caps, tracking 2, centred 120 px above the crosshair, the
  CRT pop open, 2 hard copies offset 3 px (CYAN left, DANGER right) and the word in FUSION pink / HOT, gone in 0.5 s. Read the tokens from
  `UnityTuning.Ui43.cs` (`Ui43Steel`, `Ui43Slant`, `Ui43ScanAlpha`, the motion times) and colours from `UiStyle33` / `UiTheme`.
- A1's lights: the same hue ownership (1.2); decoration only in the environment's cool hue at low intensity.
