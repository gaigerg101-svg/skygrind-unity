## What the probes showed (why each boss uses the lever it uses)
- D-1 asked for the brain's levers first. Measured, they lengthen only the bosses whose legs follow the pool: THE STAG (stagger 4 -> 9 / 14
  / 18 alone: 26.4 s) and THE WICK (stagger alone: no change). Each boss's own structure decides the lever:
  - THE STAG: one launch (320) ended a 300-HP leg, so each leg was one window; the pool makes it 2-4 windows per leg.
  - THE LOOM: its legs end when its single window closes; pool x2 = 21.7 s, nailsToTear 5 = 21.9 s. Only the meter (a parried spool on
    top of the two knees in legs 2-3) lengthens it. The pilot is sensitive: restSeconds 2 gave 4/10 killed (deaths to lashes while
    waiting for a spool), 2.5 gave 20/20. Re-check after any Loom change.
  - THE CROWN: its health is its gems (whole gems per pass); pool x2 and stagger 6 changed nothing. torchGems 1 also reached 30.5 s but
    halves the TORCH pass's reward (a verb: kept at 2). 20 gems: the view draws `c.p.gems` sockets (Crown37View.BuildCrown): a frame
    is needed (20 gems on the 3 m ring).
  - THE KILN WYRM, Stag31: pool only (AI unchanged). The worm's time steps (x2.5 48.3 s, x2.75 40.3 s): re-measure in K4's arena.
