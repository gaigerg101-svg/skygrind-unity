# NIGHT39 P1 (the pistol): notes

Builder P1, branch of port/stage3. Every number below was measured in the SimHarness (`n39pistol`) unless it says "not checked".

## The hold rule (p39, design.n39.p39; Core/Guns/Charge35.cs StepP39)
- The KESTREL only; the THUNDERCLAP keeps tap36 (n39pistol: a 60 ms THUNDERCLAP click fires on its press step).
- tapS 0.15 s (18 steps): released inside it, the tap fires on the release step; held past it, no tap, CHARGING at +18, FULL at 0.5 s.
- The tap's added latency is the click's own length: 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 / 140 ms clicks fire at +5 / +7 / +10 / +12 / +14 / +17 steps
  (42 / 58 / 83 / 100 / 117 / 142 ms). Over ~100 ms from 120 ms clicks on, so the INVERSUS correction is on (NIGHT39.md: not optional).
- The correction: the tap leaves from the press's eye along the press's aim; weight 1.00 up to 0.11 s, 0.92 at 117 ms, 0.60 at 142 ms. A
  click whose look swings 30 deg off the husk during the click: corrected, 0.72 deg off the press aim (the hip spread) and it hits; the
  correction zeroed, 29.77 deg off and it misses.
- A press while the charge cools fires on its press step (no hold can charge then): measured at +156 (0.5 s after a charged shot).
- A partial charge (0.3 s) fires the tap on its release (partialFires true).
- A held trigger: no tap first; the full charge fires itself at +179 (FULL 0.5 s + holdMaxS 1 s), then auto-fire.
- intervalMult 1.2: the KESTREL's interval 48 -> 58 steps (0.483 s), taps and the held auto-fire (StepP39 fires the held auto-fire itself).
- Off (`--off39`, or the row off): tap36 exactly. `n36tap`'s whole output is identical to the base commit's; fingerprint EQUAL with and
  without `--off39`; golden 0 mismatches, 22 replays exact.
