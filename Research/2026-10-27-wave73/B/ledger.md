## Why a strip evenly spaced in time will not show this, and what to cut instead
A flinch is a 0.12 s event; at 60 fps it is 8 frames, and the squash peak is 1 frame after the hit's frame (mirror f2: the hit frame carries the kick, the view draws -15.5 % at once). Six frames spread over 2 s land on a flinch about one time in four. Cut the frames **after the hits**: hit frame + 1 (peak) and + 5 (the stretch), which `n73-springcut.py events` prints from the telemetry, ON and OFF with the same numbers (the sim is deterministic and the row is render only; `same` checks it). With the husk framed so tightly, use `crop`. At hit + 1 the pair is 0.705 against 0.85 of the husk's height (a squash twice as deep); at hit + 5 it is 1.065 against 1.014.

### What was wrong: the reel's input, not the game
- The telemetry says the input began at f 30 (`inputFrame` 30) and the first shot's `hit` came at f 70: 40 frames later, exactly my wait loop's length (`Spring73HitWaitFrames` 40). Each gap is 69 = 40 (the loop) + 29 (the idle after it) and the kill at 452 is 41 after the 5th hit's 411. The KESTREL fires on the RELEASE of a tap and charges while held (charge35's p39: FeelReel.N71.Taps.cs, "a p39 tap (pressed two frames, released)"). My Shoot pressed it on every frame (`Fire`: pressed and held each frame) for up to 40 frames, looking for the hit event inside the hold; the hit came on the frame after the loop ended, the hold's release, and the next Shoot reset `hitFlag` to false. So `shotN` stayed empty (hence "hits landed 0", "shots at reel frames n ;") while the handler's own counter read 6 ("hit events 6"). The shots landed; the counter looked in the wrong frames. The 69-frame gap is the same cause, not a gun cooldown.
- Fix (commit below): a shot is a TAP: `UnityTuning.T125TapFrames` (3) frames held (pressed on the first), then idle frames until the hit event, up to `Spring73HitWaitFrames` 20 per try and `Spring73TapTries` 3 tries (a gun still cooling down queues the tap). The hits are recorded by the hit event's own handler (reel frame n and `FeelProbe.FrameCount` there, which is the telemetry's frame f: EndFrame bumps it at the end of that frame's RenderFrame), not by the loop; the line now prints `at reel frames n ...; telemetry frames f ...; gaps ...; aimed at 30` and the kill `at n, f`. The cadence idles `every - 1 - lastLat` frames after a hit (`lastLat` = frames from the tap's first frame to the hit), so hit to hit is 30 once a tap fires 2-4 frames after the press.
- A second thing the n73-d rows showed, and fixed in the same commit: the log has `flinch kick 6: hp 275.0 -> 1.0` and `flinch squash peak 0.325`. The reel's own `mark.hp = 1` (written from the coroutine before the killing shot) is seen by the view as a hit: a second kick on top of the fifth hit's (the rows: f 413 -0.325, against -0.154 at the first frame of a single kick). Now the lethal hp is written in the HIT EVENT handler of the fifth non-lethal hit, after its damage (the `hit` event is raised after `enemy.Damage`, and EnemyViews' own handler, registered earlier, runs first and reads the hp for its shed check): the view sees one drop on that hit's frame and kicks once. Only when no wind-up was seen does `Shoot(true)` write it itself (RED anyway).
- GREEN needs, in ON: 5 non-lethal hits recorded by the handler, the kill, a wind-up, each flinch squash at least 0.12, breath at least 0.015, drawn in more than 0 samples. Nothing about the line's verdict changed except where the counts come from.
