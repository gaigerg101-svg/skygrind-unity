## The model's assumptions

- **Its own randomness:** a Mulberry32 per run seeded from the seed (`seed * 2654435761 ^ 0x4847`), never the sim's stream. The same seed gives the same fight.
- **Aim (hit share):** each shot is fated hit or miss before it fires. The hit chance is steered so the measured share sits at 45 % hip fire and 65 % zoomed (zoomed = `loadout.adsActive` at the shot). A miss turns the look off to one side and up by missDeg while the fire button is down (starts at 20 deg; x1.5 each time a miss-fated shot hits anyway; max 90 deg, because the bosses are big and close). The move is re-projected so the feet still go where the pilot meant. Punch steps are never perturbed. The game's aim assist stays on. The measured share counts hitscan only (a `shotResult` with an enemy hit per `fire` event), and adds count as hits.
- **Trigger habit:** GG says "even while firing consistently". When the pilot has left the trigger alone for 30 steps (no fire, no alt fire, no weapon swap), is not punching, holds a gun (not the gauntlet), and the boss's aim point is within 20 deg of the look, the human clicks every 15 steps (8 Hz). `--no-trigger` turns it off.
- **Reaction (250 ms):** no pilot has a dodge that can be split out. So when a tell starts, the whole movement input (move, jump, dash, slide, grapple, reel, sprint) and the punch run 250 ms late for 1.0 s. The look and the fire are not delayed. Presses skipped at the resync merge into the resync frame. One tell in five (drawn per tell) is late by a further 200 ms.
- **The climb is not a reaction:** the first cut also delayed movement mid-climb, and THE WICK's pilot never reached the top: 0 / 3 killed in 300 s, 51 deaths per fight. Now a tell opens the late window only when the feet are on the ground and no rope is attached, and a wall ride or a rope closes an open window at once.
- **`--no-melee`:** no punch, no parry, no gauntlet. This is GG's play as he describes it, a player who has not found the melee yet.

## The sanity rule (NIGHT47.md section 4)

THE WICK dies in 72 s at the median (min 51.5 s) for the human model. That is not 40 s, but it is far from GG's "you can barely damage it". GG's read matches `--no-melee` exactly: 0 / 40 killed in 300 s at a 52 % hit share. With melee, the pilot still punches and parries perfectly inside the windows: 34 % of the bar goes in windows, at 17.8 punches a minute. For THE WICK, the honest human number lies between the two lines. W should tune against both: the default line for the band, and the no-melee line for "10 s of steady fire moves the bar >= 5 %".

## What each pilot could not model

- **All:** no zoomed shots. No pilot holds alt fire, so the 65 % cap is untested (zoom n/a everywhere). The hit share counts hitscan only, and adds count as hits. Parry and punch timing is still the bot's (only the 250 ms shift after a tell). The boss's live position in today's campaign levels is not used: wick and brawl fight on the survival map, loom on n40-span and crown on n40-spire, not on the n46 / n47 levels.
- **wick:** deaths are mortal-counted, not played out. Tells mid-climb get no delay (240 of 764 were off the ground).
- **brawl:** the hit share is 52 %, above the 45 % cap: misses at the arena's close range still hit (missDeg grows to its 90 deg cap).
- **wyrm:** the only tell is the head's charge start (Charging30). The tail sweep's tell is not keyed (the pilot's own hop handles it). The pilot swings the gauntlet with "fire", so `--no-melee` breaks it: it forces the revolver every step and the pilot never fires. That line is invalid.
- **loom:** 409 late windows were cut by the rope or ride. The late window on the ground still costs the stay pilot 17 / 40 fights at the cap (raw: 40 / 40). The model may be harsh on THE LOOM's ledge-to-rope sequences.
- **crown:** the pilot dashes and jumps between the orbit pieces almost always in the air, and 42 of 563 tells came off the ground. Its kill time hardly moves under the model (43.6 vs 37.4 s raw).

## The hook for boss builders

In your own harness file, inside `public static partial class Harness`:
`static void H47Policy<Name>(H47Ctx ctx, ref InputFrame move, ref CombatInput combat) { if (ctx.boss != "wick") return; ... }`.
It is found by reflection, and it runs after the pilot and before the human model. Set `ctx.tell = true` on the step your new attack's tell starts. Read `ctx.reacting` and `ctx.missFated`. Keep your own memory in `ctx.state`.
