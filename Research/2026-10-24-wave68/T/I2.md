# I2: the launch pilot and the launch trigger target only bodies the punch does not kill (builder T, NIGHT68)

The rule (both places: the verb pilot's launch trigger, Harness.N65.VerbPilot.cs, used by n46flow --verbs65 and every skip v2 run; and the
M3 probe `n65verb launch`, Harness.N65.Verb.cs): a target's hp must be above
`max(punch.damage x momentum.meleeMax, the largest non-parry punch dealt in this run)` = max(10 x 3 = 30, ...), read from the config.

Why "non-parry" (the lead's rule was "the largest punch seen in the run"; this narrows it, and says why):
- Measured first, shipped kb3 (Design/levels/n57-kiln.json), seeds 1-3, the probe's dash punch on 40 hp husks: 24.4 survived and launched;
  48.7 and 43.1 killed, and both were PARRIES (the punch's MeleeEvent: the body was winding up or inside parry36's early buffer).
- On NIGHT67's r3 kb3 file: 53.3 and 53.2 killed (parries), 26.9 survived and launched. Every non-parry punch dealt 24.4-26.9, under 30.
- A parry never launches (Combat.cs: `launch = launch36 != null && !parry && Wants`), so its damage says nothing about a launch punch. Had
  parries raised the bound, one parry (53) would have put every 40 hp husk under it for the rest of the run.

Prints: the verb pilot's line (V65Report and skip v2's verb-aware seed line) gives the targets as `kind hp at the trigger x count`, the
episode punches landed / killed their body / parries, the bound, the steps skipped (the nearest body in reach at or under the bound, by kind
and hp), and `no launch target` when no episode started while bodies were skipped. The probe prints the bound with both terms, each skipped
body, the chosen target's kind and hp, and `punch: dealt X vs hp Y: KILLED | survived | the punch a PARRY | not a parry`.

attacks off, and the probe prints the parry flag; the first GREEN try launched nothing because B's punch was an early parry), then the fix.
