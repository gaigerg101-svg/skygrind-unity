# I4: skip v2 pools seeds 1-20, prints the refusal count, takes the new STYLE rule (builder T, NIGHT68)

## Skip v2 (Tools/SimHarness/Harness.N65.Verb.cs V66SkipV2, Harness.N68.Skip.cs)
- Each seed's `=>` line now ends `| refused <verb> N (the refused-in run's presses refused and episodes declined), the refused-in run used
  the verb U`.
- New verdict line (the ROOM list's M2 VERB):
  `n65verb skip: M2 VERB PASS|FAIL|UNSURE | room <id>, verb <v>, pooled ratio <x> vs mainBar <b>, sets 1-10 <m> (e/10), 11-20 <m> (e/10),
  exercised N / 20 (need 14), median of all 20 seeds <y>, refusal fired on F of N exercised seeds (the refused-in run used the verb on L),
  STYLE above DULL <s> % vs need <n> % (the faster refusal <r> %: min(refusal + 15, max(refusal, 95)))[, ride band [b, b + 0.10) UNSURE] | <why>`.
  - The pooled ratio is the median of the exercised seeds' ratios over every set (NIGHT66's definition, now over 20 seeds). The median of
    all 20 seeds, exercised or not, is printed beside it. This is my reading of the ROOM list's "the median of 20"; a seed whose verb-aware
    run never used the verb does not test it.
  - The order of the verdict: UNSURE under 70 % exercised, then FAIL on the ratio, then FAIL if the refusal did not fire on every exercised
    seed, then FAIL on STYLE, then UNSURE for a ride room in [mainBar, mainBar + 0.10), else PASS.
  - The refused run's own uses of the verb (the leak) are printed but do not fail the line; the ROOM list asks for N > 0 only. I1 is the
    ride leak's fix.
- `--seed 21` and up (the GATE's fresh seeds): `n65verb skip: M2 FRESH PASS|FAIL|UNSURE | room <id>, seeds 21..30 (never tuned on),
  median <x> vs 1.25, exercised n / 10 (need 7)`.
- NIGHT67's STYLE line is kept under its own name, `n65verb skip: STYLE+15 PASS|FAIL`, so no two lines start `M2`. The M1 line and the exit
  code are unchanged: n65-verbs.sh, the GATE pack and the old reds still read M1.
- `--room-file <file>` prints `# room-file sha256: <hex> (<file>; ...)` first. The normalisation is the GATE pack's sha256n: the file's bytes
  decoded as UTF-8 with any BOM kept as U+FEFF (node keeps it), every CR LF made LF, then UTF-8 encoded, sha256, lowercase hex.
  - Checked once: C# gave 16aaf627b6aa23f8... for Logs/n67/r3-spire/room-files/v2.json, and node's sha256n gave the same.
- `--style-cases`: the STYLE rule on planted numbers, both branches on both sides of their need, with NIGHT67's rule beside each (its RED):
  `style-cases.txt` 8 / 8 GREEN. Under NIGHT67's rule, (95.5 vs 85) and (97.5 vs 97) FAIL (need 100 / 112); under the new rule they PASS.
