# I1: the ride pilot rides the line65 face only, and the ride refusal holds (builder T, NIGHT68)

## What changed (Tools/SimHarness only; no Mover.cs, no game code)
- **The refusal stops a ride before it starts.** In a refused ride run (refused-in and walker), skip v2's filter (V65Filter.NoRide68)
  gives the measured room the game's own noride rule. It calls `Mover.SetNoRide38`, the public setter NIGHT38's noride boxes and THE LOOM
  use. No ride starts on a wall inside a noride box, is drawn onto it, or turns onto it (Mover.cs NoRide38 / Ride35DrawOnto; Ride38.cs).
  - The box is the room's rect, 2 m out, at every height. The level's own noride boxes are kept.
  - Every other input is the walker's own.
  - Each wall contact in the air the rule blocked (a run of steps whose Mover counted a noride block) is one refusal: `refused ride N`.
  - The old backstop (the stick turned away once a ride started) stays as the leak detector: "rides started and ended by the away rule".
- **The verb-aware pilot rides line65's face only** (V65VerbPilot; Harness.N68.Ride.cs V68LineFace).
  - The face is the longest run of line65 segments 0-1.6 m out on one ride36 face of the room, inside its length, 3 m or more, with
    consecutive collinear segments joined. kb3c: W x 10.4 from -236.5 toward -246. g8: E x -9.4 from -228 toward -242.
  - The pilot joins anywhere on the run, from 2.5 m before its entry to 2 m before its exit. That is the scout's 2.5 m round the entry
    widened along the run: with the entry alone, kb3c seed 2's walk never came close enough. It rides in the line's direction toward the
    exit.
  - Once per pass: the pilot disarms at the episode's start, and arms again once the feet are 4 m off the run.
  - A ride room whose row has no line65 keeps the old trigger (any face of the room, toward its far end), once per pass too. This is said
    once per room.
  - line65 on no face is REFUSED, with the reason printed.
- **One pilot in all three runs** (code-scout (a2)). Outside its line episode the verb-aware pilot gives the room the same noride rule, so
  no other ride starts, the inner walker's own included. During the episode, only the episode's face is left out of the rule: a slab
  -0.3 .. +0.5 m off its plane, its length +-0.5 m, with the boxes round it stopping the ride probe + 0.05 m short. The skip line prints
  `off-line ride starts cut N`.
- Skip v2 prints each run's rides by the face they started on (`rides by face:`). This was commit 21877aac, test first.
