## R48-B1 A long tell with a wide counter (G31's third approach: the counter, not the timing)

- **ULTRAKILL parry** (ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Parry, **Verified**): parryable attacks show a **yellow flash** and unparryable ones a blue
  flash; melee attacks and enemies charging certain projectiles can be parried by the Feedbacker or a point-blank shotgun; the page gives no
  window in seconds, only "correct timing"; a parry heals the player (to the hard-damage limit), refills stamina and freezes / flashes the
  screen. Takeaway: yellow = "you can answer this now" is the genre's own language (ours: the head yellow for the whole tell, DECIDE47's
  yellow = punch it / parryable).
- **ULTRAKILL Minotaur** (ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Minotaur, **Verified**): its charge is told by a lean forward before it charges headfirst
  (plus a wind-up sound); it is parried "either normally by hitting its head or by ground slamming onto its back during the attack", i.e. the
  answer is open for the whole charge, not one frame of it; a parry deals about a third of its current health and stuns it far longer than
  a wall hit (30 vs 6 damage). Takeaway for wyrmTell48: a head-on charge whose counter is "hit the head any time it comes", with the punish
  much bigger than the dodge's, is a shipped pattern; the length of the tell then only buys reading time, it does not move the window.
- **Boss tells 1-1.5 s, punish windows longer than one combo** (Game Developer, Reported: NIGHT47 B.md R-B3, verified by the NIGHT47 lead).

## R48-B2 The burrow as the punish (G34)

- **Monster Hunter World Diablos** (monsterhunterworld.wiki.fextralife.com/Diablos, Reported: NIGHT47 B.md R-B2, verified there): it
  burrows and travels underground, resurfacing with a lunge; forced to the surface it is stunned (a safe window). Takeaway: the dive is a
  flight and a threat at once; ours makes the dive the worm's answer to being staggered.
