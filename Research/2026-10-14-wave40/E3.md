# NIGHT40 E3: the campaign roster and every enemy's look (builder notes)

Everything below was measured with `dotnet run --project Tools/SimHarness -c Release -- n40foes <cmd>` (Tools/SimHarness/Harness.N40.Foes.cs) in the
E3 worktree, 2026-10-14. No Player and no Editor run by E3. The models40 parts and the lineup40 reel are not checked on screen.

## Root causes found
- A campaign wave already spawns the DRONE, the WARDEN and the CHARGER: Campaign24.SpawnWave goes to the sim's CampaignWorld.Spawn and then to
  Husks.Spawn(kind). Director.Spawnable gates only the survival director (`n40foes spawn`).
- With the PULSE gauntlet in hand the punch is dropped (rules30 / lockout29) and the gauntlet's swing reflected nothing. A gauntlet-only player had
  no parry: 0 reflects in 251 presses (`n40foes drone`, foes40 off).
- The WARDEN's shield opened on any knock from the front, the shotgun's included. The shotgun killed a warden in 3.9-5.5 s, against 5.0 s for the
  gauntlet (`n40foes ttk`).
- The KESTREL killed a CHARGER from the front in 2.4 s. The charger also walked onto an idle player and stood there 9 s: it has no close attack, and
  inside minRange it never charges again (`n40foes trace charger`).
- The drone's hostile orb was cyan (config orb.hostileColor 0x19D3E6), which is the player's colour.
