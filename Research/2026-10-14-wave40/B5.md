## BossLine40 (for B2 and B4)

`Core/BossLine40.cs`: `new BossLine40(sim.events, sim.step)`; per fight `Begin(boss, roomId, roomIndex)`; `Say(key, text, seconds, textPad
= null, maxShows = 1)` (text is a lesson format: `{dash} {jump} {fire} {punch} {grapple}` become the bound buttons); `Step()` once per sim
step; `Clear()` at the fight's end. It emits `lessonBegin` / `lessonClear` (LessonEvent id = the boss room's id, kind `boss40`) and
`bossLine40` (BossLine30's payload). Not id "boss": hud32's BossMask32 knows no NIGHT37+ boss and drops an id "boss" line on its first frame.
