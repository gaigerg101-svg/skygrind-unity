## S1 Linear structure (R2)
- ULTRAKILL 0-1 (Into the Fire): about 11 rooms, 83 enemies, S par 2:30; weapons come from a pickup in the first arena (revolver) and a BOSS DROP (the shotgun, at 0-3); roughly one new weapon per layer; the movement kit is taught in tutorial rooms before the first real arena. Rank = time + kills + style, deaths cost points. A layer = 3 levels of 8-12 arenas, then a short boss-gated level. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/0-1:_Into_the_Fire
- V2 (1-4 Clair de Lune): the mirror boss enters a water-floor arena with breakable pillars; it uses the player's own movement; it is the difficulty spike. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/V2
- Doom Eternal arenas: "jungle gyms" (distinct floors) versus "canyons"; the resource loop (glory kill health, chainsaw ammo, flamethrower armor) keeps the player moving.
- Not found: arena sizes for 15-30 m/s, lighting and kill-box timing rules, an indie room-list format, official temporary-removal designs. INFERENCE: arenas 40-60 m across with 2-3 vertical tiers, corridors 6-8 m, 5-8 rooms and 3-5 minutes per level for a first slice.
- Slice schedule (INFERENCE): L1 pistol, the shotgun earned at the end of L1's room 2 or 3; L2 opens with a shotgun-only teaching room; one new weapon per level; restrictions (2 only, short, opt-in feeling): NO DASH for one room, PISTOL + SHOTGUN ONLY for one room; checkpoint inside each.

## S2 Shotgun fire interval (R2 section 5, a table)
Quake shotgun 0.5 s, Super Shotgun 0.7 s; Dusk about 0.9 s; ULTRAKILL base 1.25 s, Pump Charge 0.9 s; Doom 2016/Eternal not found. Ours: the web's `weapons.shotgun.fireInterval` is 0.9 s (+ `pumpDelay` 0.45): "fires too fast" means something measured on the Player is shorter than 0.9 s or reads faster (the pump, stamina or perk scales, a HEAT / MOVE flag). Measure first. A fix keeps DPS roughly constant (interval up, pellet damage scaled up).

## S3 Rewards (R3 section A)
Milestone reward every 5 rounds, bigger at 10 / 20 / 30; world pickup, no pause; ranked list: free Mystery Box spin, a guaranteed named weapon, a perk, a one-time weapon upgrade, full heal + a max-HP step, 1000-3000 points, Max Ammo, percentage cards last. At most 2 options, different in kind (3 acceptable if each is BIG); default pick if ignored; size to about 1x the best common purchase at round 5, 2-3x at round 10, 6-8x at round 20+ (CoD Zombies' 950 box / 2500 Juggernog / up to 7000 perks as the yardstick). https://callofduty.fandom.com/ (see R3) and Keith Burgun's critique of pick-1-of-3 screens (R3).

## S4 Reticle ability arc (R3 section B)
One thin arc 26-48 px below the crosshair, about 140 x 5 px at 1080p, segments for different abilities, pips (8-10 px) for charges, never both on one ability; fills left to right; dim while charging, full cyan when ready with a flash of 250 ms or less (120 ms in, 130 ms out), about 20 % alpha when idle; shape redundancy, 3:1 contrast with a dark outline, a reduce-motion option. The pixel sizes are INFERENCE. Our UI floor is 18 px text (M10) and the central 40 % rule (S14: no persistent HUD in the middle): the arc is NOT text; the S14 meter must be told it is the one allowed element (a contract line).

## S5 Charged fist (R3 section C)
Hit-fed meters precedent: Doom Eternal (glory kills feed the Blood Punch), Metal Gear Rising (fuel cells), Valorant (ult points per kill), ULTRAKILL (a parry refills stamina), Doomfist (a charged punch moves 10-23 m, cooldown 3-4 s; time-based). Recommended: 3 tiers (33 / 66 / 100 %), sources hit 2 %, fist kill 8 %, other-weapon kill 3 % (INFERENCE), full = about 25-35 kills; no time-only charge (an optional 0.2 %/s floor is NOT used: GG said no time); lose a third when hit; spent fully on the flick; boosts 0.5x / 1.0x / 2.0-2.5x the jump distance J (INFERENCE); a flick cooldown of a few seconds; one air flick until grounded or a wall touch; clamp the launch on the collider; telegraph on the gauntlet (3 vents) and on the reticle arc.

## S6 Switch hitches (R4)
Likely causes, ranked (INFERENCE from sources): `SetActive` on a large canvas (a full rebuild on re-enable: use `Canvas.enabled`), first-use shader / PSO compile (prewarm with `GraphicsStateCollection.WarmUpProgressively`), TMP dynamic glyph population (pre-fill with `TryAddCharacters`), synchronous `Resources.Load` / `Instantiate` in the click path, GC allocations. Measure: a frame-delta hitch logger (a ring buffer of unscaled deltas, log a frame above 3x the median and 8 ms with the last event tag), `ProfilerRecorder`, a cold tour then a warm tour (cold-only spikes are first-use costs), a Development Player rather than the Editor Game view (the Editor's own GUI shares the threads). Targets (INFERENCE): no hitch over 33 ms, worst cold frame under 20 ms, warm tour under 8 ms. His screenshots: gameplay 104-172 FPS, so the lag is on a switch, and the Game view was at 0.61x scale with the Stats overlay open.
