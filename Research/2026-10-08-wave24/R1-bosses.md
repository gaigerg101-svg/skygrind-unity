# R1: Boss research for a linear boss-focused SKYGRIND (2026-10-08)

Method: web only (WebSearch / WebFetch, 2026-10-08). Every sourced fact has a URL. "(mine)" marks my own design inference, not a sourced fact. "not found" means I searched and could not verify it. Wiki numbers are the current wiki values and may vary by game version or difficulty.

## 1. Segmented worm / serpent bosses

### 1.1 Terraria: Eater of Worlds (EoW)
- 67 segments (72 in Expert); EACH segment has its own HP (150 Classic, 210 Expert, 268 Master). https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/EoW
- Killing a middle segment SPLITS the worm: the neighbours become a new head and a new tail; a lone segment dies instantly. Same source.
- Head hits hardest (22 base) with the lowest defense (2); tail hits weakest (11) with the highest defense (8). Same source.
- Movement: burrows through blocks to build speed, then leaps (about 31 tiles max height) and gravity pulls it back; Expert is +20% speed and more acceleration. When far away (62.5+ tiles) it flies free. Same source.
- Expert adds 5 segments, +20% size and Vile Spit projectiles (body segments 1/900 per tick). Same source.
- How players beat it: piercing weapons, hit heads/tails only so it does not split, build a wide arena beforehand ("once the head passes a point every body segment must follow", so you stand by the path and hit each segment as it passes). Same source.
- Why it is liked: community calls it the best boss design in the game, because the split makes "tank and spank" backfire. https://terraria-archive.fandom.com/wiki/Eater_of_Worlds (search summary; fan opinion, weak source).
- Takeaway (mine): its fun is the "chain": predictable path means a segment parade you can shoot, and the split turns kills into a state change instead of a number.

### 1.2 Terraria: The Destroyer
- 82 segments sharing ONE HP pool (80,000 Classic / 120,000 Expert / 153,000 Master); segments cannot die independently. https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Destroyer
- Every segment fires "Death Lasers"; damaged segments have a 4% chance each to release a Probe (independent flying enemy that fires lasers and can be killed). Same source.
- Limited mobility in open air; it needs blocks/platforms to burrow; it chases from any distance and tries to ram and surround. Same source.
- Tips: weapons that pierce / hit many enemies; kill Probes first (they drop healing); watch the minimap to predict where it surfaces; gain height with gravity potions. Same source.
- Takeaway (mine): shared pool = no wasted shots and every hit counts, at the price of no sub-goals; the Probes add the sub-goal.

### 1.3 Terraria: Wall of Flesh (a "pursuit" boss, relevant to the chase boss)
- Slowly sweeps across the Underworld in one direction, forcing the player to keep moving; speed rises as health falls (Classic 8 mph at full to 17 mph under 10%; Expert 12 to 41 mph). https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh
- Recommended arena: a 1000+ block horizontal bridge. Solid bridges block its lasers but also your shots; platform bridges let shots through but expose you. Same source.
- Hard boundaries: touching it does 50-150 damage; leaving the Underworld triggers a tongue pull; going 187.5+ tiles away is instant death. Same source.
- Hungry (small mouths) detach at low HP and charge; Leeches spawn from the mouth. Same source.
- Takeaway (mine): the speed ramp with remaining HP is the cheap, readable difficulty curve; the arena shape (long line) is a gameplay rule.

### 1.4 Risk of Rain 2: Magma Worm
- 2400 HP (+720/level), 20 m/s, 15 armor; two stances: grounded (low, burrows quickly) and leaping (reaches higher before burrowing). https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Magma_Worm
- Leaping stance: 5 molten fireballs erupt from its body whenever it crosses a terrain boundary. Head impact is 300% damage. No documented weak point. Same source.
- Pendulum-like emerge/submerge circling the target; "Steer At Target" skill (1 s cooldown); "Blink" (10 s cooldown) to close distance. Same source.
- Advice: fight in wide open space; cramped terrain hides it ("mark" it to track it underground). Same source.
- Overloading variant: 12,000 HP, 50 damage. Same source.
- Takeaway (mine): the impact point is the danger and the fireballs on terrain crossing are the telegraph; the player's job is "be elsewhere when it lands".

### 1.5 Risk of Rain 2: Wandering Vagrant (not a worm; included as the "reading a big mass" example)
- Attacks: series of exploding orbs, a slow homing ball that detonates on impact, and a high-damage electrical nova only below 21% of max HP (can one-shot late-game). https://riskofrain.wiki.gg/wiki/Wandering_Vagrant_(Risk_of_Rain_2) (summarised from search result https://riskofrain.fandom.com/wiki/Wandering_Vagrant_(Risk_of_Rain_2))
- Takeaway (mine): a low-HP "panic" attack is a clean last-phase telegraph.

### 1.6 ULTRAKILL: Leviathan (the closest design to what we want)
- 240 HP across two parts; three phases: head only, head + tail at 75%, boat replaced by rotating midsection at 50%. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan
- Head: Lunge (35 dmg, parryable), 80 Hell Orbs in spirals with every 10th aimed at the player (25 dmg each; leaves the heart vulnerable after), Laser Sweep in phase 3 only (35 dmg, unparryable). Same source.
- Tail: High Sweep (counterclockwise, above platform) and Low Sweep (clockwise, at platform level); rotation direction telegraphs height. Same source.
- Weak point: heart on the head takes triple damage; skull reflects projectiles; mouth and body stay vulnerable. Same source.
- Arena: ship / ocean platform; at 50% the arena itself changes. Same source.
- Tactics: reflect Hell Orbs into its mouth, hit the exposed heart after the barrage, shotgun-swap into the body on lunges, use the Whiplash to mount it. Same source.
- Takeaway (mine): different body parts (head, tail) are different attack machines; the barrage is the damage window; the arena changing at 50% is the phase break.

### 1.7 ULTRAKILL: Hideous Mass
- 175 HP in 1-3 (140 + 35 across 2 phases); below 20% it enrages and flips onto its back exposing points. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Hideous_Mass
- Stationary (only rotates to face player); upright/lowered stance depends on player aggression; stone shell reflects projectiles. Same source.
- Attacks: Hell Mortars (limited homing, big explosions), Ground Slam shockwave (40), Clap vertical shockwave (20), Harpoon (very fast, severely handicaps movement). Same source.
- Weak points: abdomen and tail 3x damage and count as limbs (up to 5.25x with specific weapons). Staying on the creature disables its mortars. Same source.
- Takeaway (mine): the "stand ON it" reward is a great rule for a worm (riding the body).

### 1.8 ULTRAKILL: Cerberus (statue enemy, boss in some fights)
- 80 HP in standard boss encounters; Energy Orb (20), Dash (25, knockback), Stomp (25 shockwave); advances slowly; when one falls, all other active Cerberi enrage. https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Cerberus
- Teaches distance management and sequencing; dash has a "blue flash" tell on brutal difficulty. Same source.

### 1.9 Elden Ring: Rykard / God-Devouring Serpent
- Two phases, each with its own health bar; phase 1 is a huge snake curled in lava: poison spit, bites, slams; it hardly moves. Phase 2 is Rykard himself with a sword. https://eldenring.wiki.gg/wiki/Rykard,_Lord_of_Blasphemy
- The Serpent-Hunter weapon (blades of light, long range) makes it easier; it lies by the arena entrance. Same source.
- Not found: segment hitbox design or fight-length figures. (Dark Souls serpent bosses: not researched.)
- Takeaway (mine): a mostly stationary coil is easy to read; ranged tools exist because the boss is far.

### 1.10 Hollow Knight, Doom Eternal
- Hollow Knight: no worm boss verified; not found.
- Doom Eternal Icon of Sin: two phases; phase 1 you break 8 armor pieces with heavy weapons while it punches the arena and shoots fire; phase 2 moves to a new building and the minion spawns get stronger. https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Icon_of_Sin_(Doom_Eternal) (via search summary https://www.pocket-lint.com/games/news/bethesda/151787-how-to-beat-bosses-doom-eternal-tips-and-tricks-marauder-hunter-gladiator-khan-maykr-icon-of-sin)
- Doom Eternal Gladiator: two phases; phase 1 slow, stays behind a shield. https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Gladiator (search summary; page itself returned 403 on fetch)
- Doom Eternal Marauder: breaks flow, immune to frontal attacks while advancing with a green shield; eyes flash green before attacks, which is the counter window (Super Shotgun / Ballista stagger). https://www.doomwiki.org/wiki/Marauder and https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-NZ/news/doom-eternal-boss-tips
- Takeaway (mine): armor pieces / weak points as a visible progress bar are a proven structure.

### 1.11 Concrete spec: 3D worm boss "for SKYGRIND" (mine, built from the sources above)
Principle: the Destroyer's shared HP, the EoW's visible segment chain, the Leviathan's head/tail/phase-arena-change, the Magma Worm's "impact point + terrain crossing" telegraph.

- Segments: 24 body boxes + head + tail = 26 (EoW is 67, Destroyer 82; at a 120 Hz box sim 26 is cheap and stays readable). Segment size scales: head 3x3x4 m, body 2.5 m shrinking to 1.2 m at the tail.
- HP: ONE shared pool (Destroyer rule) so the player never wastes shots. Body hits do 1x, head 2x, a glowing "core" on the head only exposed after a roar telegraph does 3x (Leviathan heart: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan). Body segments are solid (they block, they do not die).
- Optional sub-goal (EoW split, simplified): at 66% and 33% the worm sheds its rear third as a "tail snake" with its own small pool; killing it is required to continue. Avoid per-segment HP; it creates tedious clean-up.
- Head path generation: the head follows a path of waypoints (Catmull-Rom spline) through the arena; the body is the head's recorded position history sampled every N units of distance ("snake follow", a ring buffer of head positions at fixed spacing). This is deterministic, needs no physics, and matches "every segment follows the head" (EoW source above). Use the ported Mulberry32 to pick the next waypoint pattern.
- Path patterns (a fixed cycle, so it is learnable):
  1. Spiral coil: orbit the arena at radius R, rising 1 m per revolution (a ramp the player can ride).
  2. Dive: burrow into floor, emerge from a wall 4 s later at a marked spot (ground crack + dust for 1 s: Magma Worm impact point rule, https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Magma_Worm).
  3. Strafing pass: a straight fast pass across the arena at head height (a "lunge", Leviathan: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan); the player slides under or jumps over the body.
  4. Loop-the-loop through the arena's central ring.
- Attacks (all with a colour/sound tell of at least 0.5 s):
  - Lunge: head dashes, parryable-style window optional.
  - Spit barrage from body ports (spiral pattern, every 10th aimed: Leviathan https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan).
  - Terrain-cross shards: when the head enters or leaves a wall, 5 shards burst (Magma Worm: https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Magma_Worm).
  - Body contact: low damage to avoid cheap deaths; head contact: high.
  - Phase 3 coil (squeeze): the coil radius closes until the player gets out through a gap (Wall of Flesh rule: pressure, https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh).
- Phases: P1 (100-66%) slow, learn the cycle; P2 (66-33%) tail sheds, spit starts, speed +25%; P3 (33-0%) arena shrinks or floor lowers, speed +25% (Wall of Flesh speed ramp as HP falls: https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh).
- Arena: a big cylinder, about 60 m diameter x 30 m high, with 4-6 pillars and a few platforms. Large and open because both EoW and Magma Worm are advised "wide open" (sources above).
- Movement the player is meant to use: sliding and jumping over body passes, wall-run on the cylinder wall to get above a coil, dash through gaps between segments; ride the back for safe damage (Hideous Mass rule: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Hideous_Mass).
- Avoiding cheap deaths: (a) body never one-shots (only head contact and phase-3 squeeze hurt much), (b) burrow exits are marked at least 1 s ahead, (c) never have two attacks resolve in the same 0.5 s, (d) no off-screen spawns, (e) the worm always stays at least 6 m clear of the player spawn/respawn point, (f) soft invulnerability of 0.5 s after taking a hit.
- Target fight time: 2.5-3.5 min (see section 3).

### 2.3 Keeping a chase readable at speed (partly sourced, rest mine)
- Lane/rail vs free: Crash's boulder levels are lane-like and give "little time to react" (source above). Free movement needs other readability tools.
- Distance meter (mine): a single bar showing gap to the boss; fills as you close in, and damage to the boss is gated on gap <= X. Wall of Flesh has hard limits on distance (187.5 tile death, https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh) which is a precedent for a leash.
- Rubber band (mine, with the caveat above): the boss's speed is tied to the gap but only in ONE direction (it speeds up when you are far, never slows down when you are close), and the player is told through the meter. Mario Kart's complaint is a silent rubber band (https://anildash.com/2009/04/06/elastic-happiness/ ), so make it visible.
- Speed lines (mine): GG already retired screen-border speed lines (CORE.md says "so forced... weird border"); use world-side speed cues (road markings, light streaks, boss trail) and a FOV kick instead.
- Guide line (Crash fruit-trail idea, source above): pickups or lit road strips show the intended line, dim for the advanced route.
- Telegraph with colour and shape: hazards shown 1.5-2 s ahead at speed (mine; no source).

### 2.4 Making a chase need full 3D movement (all mine, apply to a road scroll)
- Vertical layers: the road has an upper and lower deck (overpass, tunnel), the boss alternates; the player must switch via ramps or wall-jumps.
- Gaps: broken road sections of 6-14 m that need a jump / dash / slide-jump; a missing bridge piece forces a double-jump.
- Wall-run sections: a barrier wall on one side the player runs along to skip a hazard zone (a hazard on the road, clean on the wall).
- Grapple / hook anchors on overhead gantries: swinging gains speed (needs the existing move set; unverified for SKYGRIND).
- Boss attacks that require specific moves: low sweep requires a jump, high sweep requires a slide (Leviathan tail High/Low Sweep, https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan); a ground slam requires being airborne; a fired net requires a dash.
- Surface changes: oil patches reduce control, boost strips add speed, so the player picks the line.
- Boss on a different plane: it flees along the ceiling or a parallel road, forcing air movement to hit it.
- Hit window by movement: damage only counts while the player is airborne, sliding, or at a speed over a threshold ("speed pays"), so mastery is rewarded.
- Route choice: forks (risk/reward: a shortcut with gaps).

### 2.5 Concrete chase boss spec (mine)
- Track: a long straight highway streamed in 40 m tiles, recycled behind the player; boss runs ahead at base speed S_b, player max about 1.4 x S_b with movement tech.
- Gap meter: starts at 60 m. The boss can only be damaged at gap <= 25 m. At gap > 80 m the boss "escapes" (fail, restart from a checkpoint).
- Phases: P1 straight road with static hazards; P2 gaps and overpass; P3 boss drops hazards (mines, barricades) and the road tilts or splits.
- Boss attacks: rear-launched mines (jump), swung wrecking arm (slide), oil slick (avoid), a ceiling crane drop (dash out).
- HP: 6 "shield plates" on the boss, each broken by sustained shots / a melee, ending in a finisher dash into the weak point.
- Fail rule: never an instant death from one hit; being too far = soft fail.

## 3. Boss structure in linear boomer shooters

- ULTRAKILL: Leviathan 240 HP, 3 phases (https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan); Hideous Mass 175 HP, 2 phases (https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Hideous_Mass). Phases end by position or HP thresholds (75%, 50%, 20%).
- Doom Eternal: Icon of Sin 2 phases, 8 armor pieces to break, second phase on a new building (https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Icon_of_Sin_(Doom_Eternal)); Marauder is a "tests your flow" enemy (https://www.doomwiki.org/wiki/Marauder).
- Dusk: Jakob (Cult Leader) is fast and bunny-hops around the arena, hard to hit; Grand Ghoul Empress is the end boss with a "large array of attacks and high damage". https://dusk.fandom.com/wiki/Jakob?oldid=1998 and https://onsecher.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Ghoul_Empress (the second page looks like a different wiki; weak). Fight lengths and phases: not found.
- Ion Fury: boss at the end of each zone (a mech, a pair of cyborg tank twins); reviewers call them repetitive ("repeat the same tactic until HP is zero"). https://gameinformer.com/review/ion-fury/more-than-a-blast-from-the-past ; https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2020/05/14/ion-fury-review/ (search summaries).
- Severed Steel: bosses exist and players rate them "great", but details not found. https://www.mobygames.com/game/172403
- Neon White: bosses reuse normal-level skills (https://www.thegamer.com/neon-white-celeste-boss-fights-cohesive-dark-souls/).
- Fight length / HP / checkpoint numbers across these games: not found beyond the HP figures above. The 2-4 minute target is the designer's, not a sourced norm.
- "The fight teaches the move" (sourced instances): Hideous Mass teaches weak-point and positioning (https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Hideous_Mass); Cerberus teaches distance (https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Cerberus); Marauder teaches the flash-then-counter (https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-NZ/news/doom-eternal-boss-tips).
- Guidelines (mine): 2-3 phases; phases visibly change the arena or the attack set (Leviathan phase 3 swaps the arena); 2-4 min total, i.e. 60-180 s of pure damage windows; a checkpoint at each phase start; no regen mid-fight except what the kills give (GG rule: regen only at round end); arena rule stated visually (floor colour, walls).

## 4. Shortlist of 6 more boss ideas (all mine)
1. **THE LOOP (Ring Warden):** a giant torus-ring boss that rotates around a central pillar; the player runs the inside of the ring and shoots the rotating nodes. Hook: a ring that spins faster each phase. Tests: wall-run / air strafe on a curved surface.
2. **BRIDGE TITAN (Wall of Flesh style):** an advancing wall of boxes sweeping a long bridge; the player must out-run it and hit exposed eyes. Hook: "unstoppable pursuer", speed ramps with its damage (https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh). Tests: sustained speed, slide, dash.
3. **THE ORCHESTRA (Gladiator-like duelist):** a single shield-bearing knight that mirrors the player's best move; open only after the player parries its flash tell (Marauder rule: https://www.doomwiki.org/wiki/Marauder). Tests: timing, parry, close-range punishment.
4. **SKYBARGE (vertical boss):** the arena is falling debris; the boss is a floating castle; the player jumps between debris to reach turrets (Leviathan phase-3 "mount it" idea: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Leviathan). Tests: air control, grapple, long jumps.
5. **THE SWARM QUEEN (many small, one big):** a stationary queen spawning husk swarms; the queen is only vulnerable when swarm count is under N (Cerberus enrage cascade idea: https://ultrakill.wiki.gg/wiki/Cerberus). Tests: crowd control, target priority, kill rhythm.
6. **BLOCK GOLEM (rubik boss):** a boss assembled from rotating boxes; each phase it rearranges its faces so the weak point moves (Icon of Sin's armor-piece structure: https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Icon_of_Sin_(Doom_Eternal)). Tests: movement around a large target, reading rotation.

## 5. Recommendations for a 2-hour-per-boss build in Unity (C# deterministic sim, no physics engine, enemies are boxes)

General (mine):
- One `BossBase` in Skygrind.Core with: shared HP pool, phase thresholds from config, an attack scheduler that is a list of (telegraph steps, active steps, recovery steps) in whole steps (`stepsFor`), and a Mulberry32 seeded per run. No UnityEngine.Random.
- All numbers (HP, speeds, segment counts, telegraph times) in config.json via GameConfig, per CLAUDE.md rule 5.
- Hit tests are box vs ray / box vs player capsule using the existing collider. Boss pieces = list of AABBs or OBBs updated each step; no Rigidbody.
- Add fixed arena checkpoint at phase start; a restart resets Mulberry32.
- Telegraphs: a 0.5 s minimum tell (colour from the palette: red-orange for danger).
- Build order to hit 2 h each: (0:00-0:30) data + sim step; (0:30-1:15) attacks as data rows; (1:15-1:45) view (boxes only, tint on hit, health bar); (1:45-2:00) tests: deterministic replay of a fixed input script hits the same HP at step N.

Worm (priority 1; needs the follow-path code, the riskiest part):
- Core: a ring buffer of head positions (one per step), segment i reads the buffer at index `i * spacingSteps`. This is exact, cheap and deterministic. Head follows a waypoint list with a fixed turn rate; clamp pitch so it does not do a physics-free 180.
- Segments render as scaled cubes with a rotation from the segment-to-segment direction; no mesh deformation.
- Burrow = head enters a "wall AABB", the segments after it follow, visual hide when inside the wall.
- Cut scope if late: skip the tail-shed sub-boss and the squeeze coil; keep spiral, dive, lunge, spit.
- Damage: shared pool, head 2x, no per-segment HP.

Chase boss (priority 2; the road is a scroller):
- Do not move the world; keep the player moving on a fixed treadmill: the world tiles scroll at constant speed (so deterministic and no float drift) and the player's velocity is relative to that. Alternative: move the player on a long straight and recycle tiles behind.
- Gap meter = player.z minus boss.z. Implement the boss as scripted speed with a speed ramp tied to HP lost (Wall of Flesh ramp, https://terraria.wiki.gg/wiki/Wall_of_Flesh).
- Hazards are authored in a seeded sequence list per phase, each with a type, lane x, and spawn z.
- Include 2 vertical features (a gap + an overpass) so it is not left/right only (section 2.4).

Third boss (priority 3): pick **THE LOOP** or **BRIDGE TITAN** from section 4; BRIDGE TITAN shares the chase boss's scroller code (the same treadmill reversed), so it is the cheapest second use of that work.

Open questions for GG (mine): which JoJo fight (section 2.1); whether the chase is "player chases boss" or "boss chases player"; whether the grapple exists in the move set.
