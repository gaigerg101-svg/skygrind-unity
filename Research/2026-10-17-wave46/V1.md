## 1. Input to view, per verb (120 Hz: 1 step = 8.33 ms; 0 = the press's own step)

The frame loop polls input first, steps the sim, then draws (SurvivalGame.cs:280-345). Mover events fire inside the step, and their
camera / hands kicks are drawn that same frame. The look (yaw / pitch) goes straight to the camera every frame and is never
interpolated (SurvivalGame.Render.cs: `feel.Apply(cam, drawn, ..., input.yaw + ..., input.pitch + ...)`). The feet are drawn
interpolated between the last two steps, so they trail the sim by less than 1 step. The F7 bar is 12 steps (100 ms).
