Card: NIGHT46.md F1. Files: `Tools/SimHarness/Harness.N46.Flow.cs` (the command), `Tools/night/n46-flow-geo.cjs` (the geometry: FLOW-AUDIT's

## The command (for every builder)

```
dotnet run --project Tools/SimHarness -c Release -- n46flow --level <id> [--seeds 10] [--first 1] [--rooms a,b] [--rows r1,r2] [--off46]
    [--cap 240] [--bosses] [--no-geo] [--per-seed] [--out file] [--trace]
```
- `--level <id>`: the map file `Design/levels/<id>.json` played as the campaign with the config's rows (your n46 level loads as itself;
  older systems read it through BaseId). `--level all` = the five ON levels below. A placeholder plays exactly as its base: `n46-span`
  seed 1 equals `n40-span` seed 1 in every room number (measured).
- `--rooms a,b`: measure only these rooms; the walk starts at the first (DOORFREE for the placement only, as `n45yard walk`) and ends
  when the last clears. Without it: every `kind: room` from the level's first fight room to its last.
- `--rows r1,r2`: forces NIGHT46+ sim rows on (`sim.rowsOverride = rowsRegistry.Defaults().Set(r, true)`; an unknown name is refused).
  `--off46`: NIGHT45's game. The command applies the off switches itself (it runs from a module initializer, before Program.Main).
- `--set design.n46.<row>.numbers.<key>=<n>`: a number changed in memory only (a probe; never written). 
- `--bosses`: also measure boss rooms (see the pilot); each boss room prints its Reveal and leg / phase-change seconds. `--per-seed`: one line per room per seed. `--trace`: the pilot's detours.
- Same seeds, same build = the same numbers (re-run of n40-span and n45-iceline, seeds 1-10: byte-identical output).
- **Runtime** (10 seeds, 24-core laptop, 10 runs in parallel, other builders' jobs on the machine): 70-117 s per level (span 70, spire
  72, iceline 89, kiln 99, foundry 117 s). Under 3 min. Run it the same way before and after (same seeds, same `--rooms`).

## The pilot (one for every room; a fault is the pilot's)
- Walk38Pilot round the ported autopilot brain (`BotNav.Survival`, persona normal), as `n43level walk` / `n45yard walk` drive it, plus
  ONE rule read from level data, never a room id (the autopilot's graph has no lava; round 0: the plain pilot died 18 times in a5 and
  stopped there): in lava, out by the nearest rim; never step into unbridged lava (stand at the edge and fight); held at an edge 2 s,
  a 3 s grid path round the lava to the nearest enemy above ground (none above ground: back to the room's spawn); a cleared room whose
  straight line out crosses lava, a grid path round it. Links and bosses keep the walk pilot's own frame.
- A fight room not cleared in 240 s ends that seed: printed as `cap`, and **left out of that room's numbers** (the pilot's fault).
- A link not crossed in max(30 s, 3 x par), or a cleared room not left in 15 s: the pilot is put at the next room's spawn (counted in the
  header line; HP restored to the link's entry value). Links are not measured.
- **Bosses are not measured by default**: the walk pilot killed neither o3 THE LOOM nor v11 THE CROWN in 240 s (probe, seed 1), x9 cleared
  at 0.0 s, k5 was not reached. Each boss has its own pilot (B1: wyrm46's harness for k5 / THE KILN WYRM).

## The metrics (per fight room, and per level)
In-fight = the room's `roomEnter` to its `roomClear`, the retry pause left out.
| metric | definition |
|---|---|
| stall % | in-fight steps with horizontal speed < 25 % of `ground.walkSpeed` (8 m/s: under 2 m/s) |
| longest stall | the longest run of stalled steps (s): per room the median over seeds; per level the worst room's median |
| air % | in-fight steps not grounded |
| kill gap | s between two kills in the room: median / max (pooled over seeds) |
| near | the nearest live enemy's horizontal distance (m) on the steps one lives: median / p10 / p90 |
| dwell | s within 4 m of the room's entry door rect (the first room: its spawn) while an enemy lives (median over seeds) |
| dead | s in the fight with no enemy alive and the door still shut: wave gaps, teaching holds, the commit gate, start40 (median) |
| deaths / falls | room retries; falls = stageRespawn + fallReturn43 (totals over the seeds) |
| clear | roomEnter -> roomClear (s, median); k/n cleared, `cap c` = rooms the pilot did not clear |
| geometry | floor m2 (walkable, lava out), tiers, loop y when the walk graph's largest hole >= 50 m2 (FLOW-AUDIT's y rooms 105-177 m2, edge-only <= 22.6), pockets, the longest sightline, wave 1's nodes in view from the spawn (<= 53 deg and a clear line) / all |
Level dwell / dead / clear are the sums of the room medians.

### The g7 fix (the lead's ask, 00:00): overlap46 holds two kinds of wave, both general rules in its numbers
- `entryM 12`: a wave whose OWN nodes (waveNodes43) put one within 12 m of the room's spawn (the entry) waits for the full clear. In levels
  1-5 it catches exactly g7's wave 3. Alone it did NOT fix g7 (overlap46 alone: still 8 deaths): `--trace` showed the deaths come with
  **wave 2's seven released on top of wave 1's last spitter**, not with wave 3.
- `growthMax 2`: a wave more than 2 x the wave before it (the room's next beat) waits for the full clear. It holds g7 wave 2 (3 -> 7) and
  g3 wave 2 (3 -> 8); every other wave keeps its overlap. Raw: `Research/2026-10-17-wave46/F1-rows-fix.txt`.
- g7 deaths in 10 walks: baseline 0 (seeds 1-10) / 4 (seeds 11-20); overlap46 before the fix 8; after: **overlap46 alone 1**, the four rows
  **1** (seeds 1-10) and **6** (seeds 11-20, against the baseline's 4 on the same seeds: inside the band of 4). The 11-20 deaths come after
  wave 3 (the door beat), which the fix leaves as the room's design.
