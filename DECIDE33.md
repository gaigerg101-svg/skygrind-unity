# DECIDE33: the defaults NIGHT33 builds on (GG can reverse any; each is one design.n33 row or number)

- **D-1 HUD language:** no panel fills; one motif, the 20-degree slash; text with a 2 px shadow; bone-white points; one top-centre message slot (U1).
- **D-2 Boss length (again):** pilot band 15-22 s (a person about 40 s) after "still way too tanky" with 26-39 s pilot fights.
- **D-3 The whip:** light enemies come to you, heavier ones pull you and you stop 1.5 m short with a small hit; rings and boss joints stay pull targets; a whip kill gives no grapple charge back.
- **D-4 The piercer:** the charged KESTREL shot pierces everything in a line at 8-10x damage with a beam, 4 s cooldown.
- **D-5 Zoom:** world FOV only; the gun lowers slightly and never moves to the centre.
- **D-6 Points:** carried across the campaign and spent in a shop between levels on eight upgrades (two weapon mods, one movement perk, one wildcard).
- **D-7 Lava rooms:** their first wave waits for a trigger line on the far side.
- **D-8 Environment art:** procedural box-built props and shader surfaces, no imported kits (CC0 kits exist; boxes keep the look one style and need no licence check).
