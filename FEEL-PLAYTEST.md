# FEEL-PLAYTEST (NIGHT41): the finish night, about 15 minutes, pad first

Build: `Builds/n41-r7/SKYGRIND.exe` (the NIGHT41 freeze; NIGHT41-REPORT.md has every row). `-skygrindOff41` gives NIGHT40's game for a
side-by-side (NIGHT40's walk is in git history: `git show u3-n40:FEEL-PLAYTEST.md`). Pick levels from LEVEL SELECT.
The one question for each: **does it look and feel better than `-skygrindOff41`, and does it answer what you asked?**

1. **The shotgun (1 min, level 3).** Fire the THUNDERCLAP a few times: is the pump quick enough now (done in under half a second)?
2. **Level 2's look (2 min).** Does it read as an ice canal at dusk now (cold blue-white light, frost along the wall bases)? Then level 3
   (molten amber), level 4 (dusk violet), level 5 (pale gold): better than one warm light everywhere?
3. **A boss entrance (any boss, 1 min).** Walk into a boss room: the name card, a shake and a CRT flicker, then the boss's rule in one
   beat. Too much, too little? Take a hit from a boss: does the "TV" static read (it is short)?
4. **The boss bar (any fight).** One bar: ticks at the phases (pips for THE LOOM and THE CROWN), the STAGGER meter under it, OPEN in cyan
   while a window counts down. Do you know where the fight is?
5. **Doors (level 1, 1 min).** Enter a room: the door ahead locks as a red-orange holographic sheet until the room is clear, then turns
   cyan. Readable? The boss door has a pale violet-white frame.
6. **THE ICEBREAKER (level 2, 3 min).** Does it look like ice armour now (blue plates, red-orange fists in the tells, a cyan stuck fist)?
   Does the gauntlet feel heavier on it and on tanks / WARDENs / CHARGERs (a short freeze and a bigger knock)?
7. **THE LOOM (level 4, 3 min).** Watch for the red-orange sheet over the canyon floor: get high (a wall, the hook, a tower) before the
   line crosses. In phase 2 it covers the towers: hook. Fair warning?
8. **Fights (any level).** No more than two enemies wind up at once now; the rest circle. Busier or calmer than before?
9. **Level 5 (2 min).** Grapple rings in the two arena rooms (5.2 THE NAVE, 5.6 THE CHOIR, with two DRONEs): do they help the descent?
10. **Level 1's GANTRY (1 min).** The long wall no longer carries you across alone: chain the walls. Do you notice where the ride stops?
